[MEC] Transport and Installation#

Important

Lifting and handling operations must be carried out exclusively by specialized and trained personnel qualified to perform such activities.

The machine has been designed so that a forklift of adequate capacity is required during packing, transport and assembly. Please note that the machine has no dedicated attachment points (for example: eyebolts) for lifting.

Packaging#

The machine is shipped by ARS s.r.l. from the production plant to that of the end-user Customer.

  • Standard shipping method:

  • Normal protective packaging: Suitable for short and medium distances.

  • Special protective packaging: Suitable for long distances.

Note

Transport note: Shipment must be carried out with covered or tarpaulin-covered vehicles depending on the type of load.

Receiving and unpacking the machine#

Upon receipt of the machine, the customer must check that there is no damage caused by the transport method or by the personnel in charge. Scrupulously follow the procedures below:

In case of ascertained damage:
Leave the package in question in the condition in which it was found and immediately request the assessment of the damage by the competent shipping company. Then, report the damage found with a damage certificate to the competent transport insurance and to the point of sale.
Standard unpacking procedure:
If the machine is delivered in a crate on a pallet or wooden brackets with possible heat-shrink cellophane protection, first remove the cover. To free the machine completely, remove the screws and the metal strapping. Then lift the machine using a crane or forklift (as described in the technical data) and remove the pallet used for transport.

Handling with packaging#

HANDLING OF THE MACHINE BODY WITH PACKAGING#

Operator qualification

Lifting equipment operator

PPE required

Lifting equipment

Forklift with a capacity of not less than 50 kg

Attention

Use only suitable and approved lifting equipment, compatible with the dimensions and weight of the machine.

Attention

Make sure that nobody stands underneath or within the operating range of the lifting equipment.

To handle the machine body with packaging, proceed as described:

1
Position the forks of the forklift under the wooden crate in which the machine is stored.
2
Make sure that the forks protrude from the front of the load (at least 5 cm), for a length sufficient to eliminate any risk of the transported part tipping over.
3
Raise the forks until they contact the load.
Note: if necessary, secure the load to the forks with clamps or similar devices.
4
Slowly lift the load by a few tens of centimeters and check its stability, making sure that the center of gravity of the load is positioned at the center of the lifting forks.
5
Tilt the mast backwards (towards the driver's seat) to counter the tipping moment and ensure greater stability of the load during transport.
6
Adjust the transport speed according to the flooring and the type of load, avoiding abrupt maneuvers.

Attention

Position the forks of the forklift as shown in the figure.

Removing the packaging#

To remove the packaging, proceed as described:

1
Position the machine in its designated location.
2
Remove the strapping from the base of the wooden crate used for shipping.
3
Grasp the hopper by its base, not by the bowl, to lift it and remove it from the crate.

Attention

Two operators are required for manually lifting the hopper out of the wooden crate.

For the handling of the machine and/or its parts, refer to the “Transport and Handling” paragraph.

Disposal of packaging#

The packaging is an integral part of the supply and is not taken back, therefore its disposal is the responsibility of the purchaser. Any disposal or destruction must be carried out in compliance with the regulations in force in the user’s country, taking into account the nature of the materials:

  • Wood: for the crates;

  • Plastic film: for protecting the machine, and adhesive tapes for fastening;

  • Bags of absorbent substance: for protection from humidity;

  • Other residual elements (e.g. straps, nails, etc.).

Transport and Handling#

Depending on the transport method, ARS s.r.l. uses packaging and fastenings suitable to guarantee integrity and preservation during transport.

Upon receipt of the machine, check that no part has been damaged during transport and/or handling. If any anomalies or damage are found, they must be reported to the Manufacturer immediately.

Note

Note on qualifications: The handling activities described in this paragraph must be carried out exclusively by personnel qualified for such operations: personnel specifically trained to safely perform loading, unloading and handling operations using lifting equipment, and who are aware of the accident-prevention rules.

Attention

Never lift the unit by the bowl or the moving part, as besides going out of calibration it could also suffer deformation or breakage.

Attention

ARS S.r.l. is not liable for damage to property or persons caused by accidents resulting from failure to comply with the instructions given in this manual.

Liability note: All the required technical specifications are contained in the sales contract. The Manufacturer declines all liability if the customer does not guarantee the agreed technical characteristics of the electrical system.

Permitted environmental conditions#

The environment in which the machine is installed and used must be indoors and protected from atmospheric agents (rain, hail, snow, fog), airborne or combustible dust, corrosive vapors or sources of excessive heat. The area must not be classified as ATEX.

In particular, the installation and use environment must not present:

  • Exposure to corrosive fumes or oily vapors;

  • Exposure to excessive humidity (above 85 %) and rapid changes in relative humidity (above 0.005 p.u./h);

  • Exposure to excessive or abrasive dust;

  • Exposure to explosive mixtures of dust or gas, or to salty air;

  • Exposure to abnormal vibrations, impacts or shocks;

  • Exposure to high or rapid temperature variations (above 5 K/h) or nuclear radiation.

Installation Area#

Attention

Never lift the unit by the moving assembly, as the vibrator has been calibrated at the factory according to your specific requirements and could go out of calibration.

The unit must not be installed where the channel may come into contact with rigid objects or adjacent surfaces; maintain a clearance of about 3-5 cm between the vibrating part and the surrounding static parts.

Hopper positioning#

For correct positioning, follow this operating sequence:

1
Place the hopper on a stable surface.
Note: if installed on the platform of a machine sensitive to vibrations, interpose insulating and anti-vibration material between the surfaces.

2
Fasten the hopper through the dedicated holes.
Note: the standard hopper has 4 holes in the base for M8 screws for fastening to the support surface.
3
Proceed with the necessary connections, referring to the dedicated paragraph.

Attention

In the case of custom models, the maximum allowable load and the throughput may differ from the values indicated. For models of this kind, the Manufacturer provides the data in question.

Attention

Make sure that the support surface of the machine is flat and horizontal and is suitable to support its weight.

Connections#

To put the machine into operation, the necessary connections to the local networks must be ensured:

  • Electrical connection (including the earthing connection).

It is the sole responsibility of the user to guarantee the required connection characteristics.

Attention

The required connections must be made by qualified and authorized personnel.

Electrical Connection#

Attention

Before performing any electrical connection operation, it is important to check that the machine is switched off.

Attention

Make sure that the customer’s electrical power supply line has been disconnected beforehand.

The compliance of the connection between the machine and the earthing system is entirely the purchaser’s care and responsibility.

Attention

The operation must be carried out exclusively by specialized and authorized personnel (electrical maintenance technician).

Before proceeding with the electrical connection, check that:

  • The technician is aware of the regulations in force in the country of installation;

  • The frequency and supply voltage values correspond to those of the local network;

  • The cross-section of the electrical cables used is adequate for the current draw and maximum loads of the machine;

  • The circuit earthing fully complies with the EN 60204-1 standard;

  • The materials used in the earthing system have adequate strength or mechanical protection.

Attention

Do not operate with wet hands or objects. In case of fire, do not use water on electrical components.

ELECTRICAL CONNECTION - AC#

Operator qualification

Electrical maintenance technician

PPE required

To connect to the electrical mains - AC, proceed as described below:

1
Connect the system to the mains power supply.
2
Carefully check that the earthing conductor is correctly installed and intact.

Important

The standard power supply is 115 VAC or 230 VAC +/-5% (the 115 VAC variant is supplied only on explicit request).

Attention

An incorrect power supply can cause serious electronic anomalies in the system and prevent its correct operation.
