Operator Profiles and Qualifications#

Operator
User's personnel trained and authorized to operate the machine for production purposes. Performs the operations necessary for proper operation and for their own safety. Has proven experience and specific training. In case of doubt, reports anomalies to their superior.
⚠ Not authorized to carry out any maintenance activity.
Mechanical Maintenance Technician
Technician qualified for preventive/corrective activities on mechanical parts. Accesses all parts of the machine for visual analysis, adjustment and calibration. Reads pneumatic/hydraulic diagrams, technical drawings and spare parts lists. In extraordinary cases may operate with reduced safety devices.
⚠ Not authorized to work on live electrical systems.
Electrical Maintenance Technician
Technician qualified for preventive/corrective maintenance on the electrical system. Reads the electrical diagrams and verifies the functional cycles. May work on live electrical panels only if holding PEI qualification (Standard EN 50110-1).
⚠ Does not perform software programming (PLC or safety logic) and does not change passwords.
Software Expert Technician
Manufacturer's technician with proven experience on PLC/PC systems and drives. Performs changes to machine data, creation of work programs and adjustment of drive parameters. May work on live electrical panels only if a PEI-qualified person (EN 50110-1).
Manufacturer's Technician
Technician specialized and qualified directly by ARS S.r.l. (or by the distributor) for highly complex operations. Knows the construction cycle of the machine in detail and intervenes at the specific request of the user.

All the above qualifications necessarily fall within the category of “Trained Person”:

Trained Person
A person informed, instructed and trained on the work and on the hazards arising from improper use. Knows the importance of safety devices, accident-prevention rules and the operating conditions for working safely.

Safety Area Mapping#

Control zones
Areas in which the operator controls the cyclic functions (in automatic or semi-automatic mode) by means of the dedicated panels.
Maintenance / adjustment zones
Accessible only to qualified mechanical and electrical maintenance technicians for setting operations. They remain off-limits during the standard production cycle.
Danger zones
Areas inside or around the machine with residual risks. Access is categorically forbidden to anyone when the machine is running.

Warning

ACCESS TO DANGER ZONES

The risks in the machine zones are protected by physical guards (covers, doors) and interlocked electrical devices (sensors, microswitches).

When the system is active it is strictly forbidden to:

  • climb over the guards;

  • bypass the safety devices;

  • intervene inside the protected areas.

Removing or circumventing the safety systems may cause serious injury to persons and damage to the machine.

Safety#

Noise

Measurements carried out in accordance with UNI EN 11200 and UNI EN ISO 3746. Noise exposure during operation is 90 dB. The actual level on site may vary depending on the type of installation, the adjacent machines and the characteristics of the environment.

Vibrations

The vibrations produced by the machine under normal operating conditions are not hazardous to the health of the operators. Excessive vibration is a symptom of mechanical failure and must be reported and eliminated immediately.

Electromagnetic Compatibility

The machine complies with the EMC Directive. Emission values fall within the regulatory limits thanks to the use of certified components, suitable connections and filters where necessary. Non-compliant maintenance work or incorrect replacement of components may compromise this compliance.

Attention

MANDATORY — Personal protective equipment must be used while the machine is operating.

Residual Risks#

The machine has been designed to guarantee the essential safety requirements; however, residual risks remain in the following phases:

  • transport and installation

  • normal operation

  • adjustment and setup

  • maintenance and cleaning

  • disassembly and dismantling

Hazards due to handling
The operations of unloading, opening the packaging and handling the machine expose operators to the risk of suspended loads and crushing. They must be carried out exclusively by personnel specialized in operating lifting equipment and specifically trained.

The detailed procedures are described in the "Transport and Installation" chapter.
Electrical hazard
Access and maintenance operations expose to electrical risk. Work on live parts must be carried out exclusively by expert and qualified personnel. Safety measures:
  • Comply with the safety pictograms relating to electrical risk.
  • Do not perform maintenance without first disconnecting the power supply.
  • Consult the manuals of the commercial equipment for specific recommendations.
  • Periodically inspect the equipotential protection circuit.
Hazard from dust and splinters
At the end of the work cycle, residues of the fed parts or accumulations of dust may remain on the surface of the machine. Carefully clean the vibrating surface after each use (see chap. 7).

Attention

Do not carry out maintenance and cleaning activities without first de-energizing all the energy sources present.

Attention

It is absolutely forbidden to remove the safety guards or open the fixed guards without first disconnecting the electrical and pneumatic power supply of the machine.

The user is required to:

  • analyze the handling and installation risks within their own premises;

  • raise awareness among and instruct the personnel assigned to the operations;

  • apply visual safety signs in the working environment.
