[MEC] Machine Description#

The hoppers are particularly suitable for feeding and pre-dosing small, medium and large parts. They are driven by a linear vibrating base, whose model varies according to the size of the hopper itself.

Components and Operation#

  • Base structure: The linear vibrating base is made up of two bodies joined together by leaf springs.

  • Vibration mechanism: Operation relies on an electromagnet integral with the fixed body that attracts and releases the moving body. This action generates the vibration that allows the parts contained inside the bowl (positioned on the moving body and locked by means of clamps) to advance.

  • Electrical control: To fulfil this purpose the vibrators require a controller that converts alternating current into pulse current, as well as adjusting the speed of the system itself.

Intended Use#

The hopper is available in six base models. Custom models, derived from the base ones but different in terms of size and performance, can be made after discussion of the user’s requirements.

Note

Validity note: The information contained in this manual is also to be considered valid for custom models.

Intended Purpose

The machine was created to:

  • meet the specific requirements mentioned in the sales contract;
  • be used according to the instructions and limits of use given in this manual.

Safety Conditions

The machine is designed and built to work safely only if:

  • it is used within the limits declared in the contract and in this manual;
  • the procedures of the user manual are scrupulously followed;
  • routine maintenance is carried out at the times and in the ways indicated;
  • extraordinary maintenance is promptly carried out when necessary;
  • the safety devices are NEVER removed and/or bypassed.

Misuse (Reasonably Foreseeable)#

  • Processing liquid components and fine grit;
  • Changing working parameters that compromise safety;
  • Transporting people;
  • Using the machine as a support point;
  • Using the machine so as to obtain production values higher than the prescribed limits;
  • Modifying / tampering with the electrical and pneumatic connections of the machine or any other of its components;
  • Using the machine with a product other than the one listed in the “Intended use (correct)”;
  • Using the machine differently from what is provided for in the “Intended use (correct)” paragraph.

Warning

Any use of the machine other than that intended must be authorized in writing in advance by the Manufacturer. In the absence of such written authorization, the use is to be considered “improper use”; therefore the Manufacturer declines all liability for any damage caused to property or persons and considers any type of warranty on the machine void.

Important

Improper use of the machine excludes any liability of the Manufacturer.

Obligations and Prohibitions#

Obligations of the users#

The user (entrepreneur or employer) must:

  • Take into account the capabilities and conditions of the operators in relation to their health and safety;
  • Provide personal protective equipment (PPE) suitable for the individual procedures;
  • Provide lifting equipment and procedures that comply with the standards;
  • Require each worker to comply with the company rules and provisions on safety and on the use of the collective and personal protective equipment made available to them;
  • Instruct the personnel on the procedures to follow in the event of an accident;
  • Instruct the personnel on the residual risks present;
  • Instruct the personnel on the devices provided for operator safety;
  • Instruct the personnel on the risks of noise emission in the working environment;
  • Instruct the personnel on the general accident-prevention rules laid down by European directives and by the legislation of the country of destination of the machine.

Note

Allow only personnel who have read this manual and have been properly trained to operate the machine.

Obligations of the assigned personnel (operators/maintenance technicians/technicians)#

The personnel must:

  • Always carry out maintenance work with the machine switched off. Never lubricate moving parts;
  • When the machine is running, avoid working nearby wearing chains, bracelets, ties or other garments that may get caught in the mechanisms;
  • Long hair must be tied back to avoid the risk of entanglement;
  • Carry out work on the electrical panel, junction boxes, cables and electrical components only with the main switch off;
  • Make sure that no one is inside the danger zones before starting the machine;
  • Pay the utmost attention during operation so that no one can directly access the moving parts;
  • Use the protective devices provided by the employer appropriately;
  • Immediately report to the employer, manager or supervisor any deficiency or anomaly in the safety devices.

Prohibitions for the assigned personnel#

In particular, the personnel must not:

  • Use the machine improperly (i.e. for uses other than those indicated in the “Intended Use” paragraph);
  • Remove or modify the safety or signalling devices without prior authorization;
  • Carry out on their own initiative operations or maneuvers outside their competence or that may compromise their own safety or that of other workers;
  • Wear bracelets, rings or chains that may dangle and be dragged by moving parts;
  • Replace or modify the speeds of the machine components without the explicit consent of a supervisor;
  • Modify the programmed cycle of the machine in any way;
  • Modify the electrical connections with the aim of excluding or bypassing the internal safety devices;
  • Use the machine if it has not been installed in full compliance with the regulations in force;
  • Use the machine as a support point, even if not operating (risk of the operator falling and/or damage to the structure);
  • Use the machine outside the permitted environmental conditions (see “Chapter 5”).

Attention

ARS S.r.l. is not liable for damage caused to property or persons in the event that:
• it is found that the machine has been used in one of the non-permitted environments;
• the obligations and prohibitions described here have not been complied with.

Processing cycle#

The processing cycle, divided into the following operating phases, is described below in a simplified manner:

1
The operator places the product to be processed inside the bowl, manually or with an automatic loading system.
2
The hopper cyclically performs vibrations (set by the operator) to allow the parts to advance, so as to constantly ensure their presence on the feeder.
