[MEC] Accessories and Options#

This page lists the variants, accessories and options available in the price list for the vibratory hoppers. Each price list item has its own section, reachable through a direct link.

Attention

Unless otherwise specified, accessories and options are not available for the 1.5 l hopper. For custom models, refer to the project technical file.

Hopper Variants#

110 V Version#

Hopper version for low-voltage power supply networks. The standard hopper power supply is 230 Vac ±5%; the low-voltage variant is supplied only on explicit request.

Power supply

115 Vac ±5%

Frequency / Phase

50-60 Hz / single-phase

Anti-Vibration#

If the hopper is installed on the bed of a vibration-sensitive machine, insulating and anti-vibration material must be placed between the two surfaces, to prevent the hopper vibrations from being transmitted to the FlexiBowl®. See [MEC] Transport and Installation.

Controller Cables#

Note

The FlexiBowl® connection cables (power supply, Ethernet, I/O, rack) are described on the Connection Cables page.

Modbus RTU Cable#

RS485 (Modbus RTU) communication cable between the hopper controller and the FlexiBowl®. It connects to the HOPPER connector of the FlexiBowl® (standard panel for sizes 500 – 1200, rack for sizes 200 / 350) and to one of the two RS485 ports of the controller; the other RS485 port allows daisy chain connection between multiple hoppers.

FlexiBowl® side connector

M12, A-code

Controller side connector

5-pin (RS485 port)

References

Pinout and connection sequence: [ELE] Controller and Wiring

Important

In single-hopper configuration, the unused RS485 connector must be closed with the dedicated termination cap, fitted with a 120 Ω resistor between A+ and B-.
