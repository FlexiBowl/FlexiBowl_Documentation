Transport and handling#

Packaging#

The machine is shipped by ARS s.r.l. from the production plant to that of the user Customer. Depending on the transport distance, the specific requests of the Customer, and the time the load remains in its packaging, the machine is shipped in the following ways:

  • normal protective packaging for short and medium distances;

  • special protective packaging for long distances.

Shipping must be carried out with covered or tarpaulin-covered transport vehicles, depending on the type of load.

Inspection on arrival#

Note

Upon receipt of the product, before signing the delivery note:

  1. External inspection of the packaging: Check the integrity of the box and of the outer packaging. Check for any signs of impacts, crushing or wetting.

  2. Contents check: Compare the contents with the delivery note. Check that all the ordered components are present.

In case of damage or discrepancies#

Note

If problems are found:

  • Do NOT sign the receipt as “compliant”

  • Note the damage on the transport document

  • Photograph any obvious damage

  • Contact the ARS service department immediately: service@arsautomation.com us.service@arsautomation.com if contacting from America

Transport and handling of the packaging#

Tip

During transport:

  • Always handle the packaging in an upright position (observe the “UP” arrows on the packaging)

  • Do not drop or knock the package

  • Use trolleys or pallet trucks suited to the weight

  • Avoid sudden temperature changes

Operator qualification

Required PPE

Lifting equipment

Lifting equipment operator

Forklift with a capacity of not less than 50 kg

Warning

  • Use only suitable and approved lifting equipment, compatible with the dimensions and weight of the FlexiBowl®.

  • Make sure that nobody is standing within the operating range of the lifting equipment.

To handle the machine body with its packaging, proceed as described:

Step

Operation

1

Position the forks of the forklift under the wooden crate in which the machine is placed.

2

Make sure that the forks protrude from the front of the load (at least 5 cm), for a length sufficient to eliminate any risk of the transported part tipping over.

3

Raise the forks until they contact the load.

Note

If necessary, secure the load to the forks with clamps or similar devices.

4

Slowly lift the load by a few tens of centimeters and check its stability, making sure that the center of gravity of the load is positioned at the center of the lifting forks.

5

Tilt the mast backwards (towards the driver’s seat) to counteract the tipping moment and ensure greater stability of the load during transport.

6

Adjust the transport speed according to the flooring and the type of load, avoiding abrupt maneuvers.

Storage#

Tip

During storage:

  • Store in a dry and covered place

  • Do not stack other loads on the packaging

  • Do not climb on or lean against the packaging

  • Observe the environmental conditions indicated in the previous table

Unpacking#

Tip

During unpacking:

  • Open carefully so as not to damage the internal components

  • Keep the original packaging for any returns or future transport

  • Check that all accessories and documentation are present

To remove the packaging, proceed as described:

Step

Operation

1

Place the FlexiBowl® in its intended location.

2

Unscrew the FlexiBowl® from the base of the wooden crate used for shipping.

3

Remove the USB device and the test file from the crate. Keep them for later use.

4

Lift the FlexiBowl® and remove it from the crate.

Warning

Two operators are required for the manual lifting of the FlexiBowl® out of the wooden crate.

To handle the machine and/or its parts, refer to the paragraph “Transport of the FlexiBowl®”.

Disposal of the packaging#

The packaging is an integral part of the supply and is not taken back, so its disposal is the responsibility of the purchaser. Any disposal or destruction must take place in compliance with the regulations in force in the user’s country, taking into account the nature of the materials:

  • wood for the crates;

  • plastic film for protecting the machine and adhesive tapes for securing it;

  • bags of moisture-absorbing substance;

  • etc.

Transport of the FlexiBowl®#

ARS s.r.l., depending on the transport method, uses packaging and fixings suitable to guarantee integrity and preservation during transport.

Upon receipt of the machine, check that no part has been damaged during transport and/or handling. If any damage is found, it is mandatory to report it immediately to the Manufacturer.

The handling activities described in this paragraph must be carried out by personnel qualified for such operations: personnel specifically trained to carry out loading, unloading and handling operations safely using lifting equipment, and who are aware of the accident prevention rules.

Warning

ARS S.r.l. is not liable for damage to property or persons caused by accidents resulting from failure to comply with the instructions given in this manual.

Warning

The standard EN ISO 11228-1-2022 prohibits manually lifting loads exceeding 25kg (the limit may be even lower depending on the sex and age of the operator); therefore, for safety reasons, the use of lifting machinery is mandatory for FlexiBowl® units that exceed this limit. The FlexiBowl® units that exceed the 25kg limit are those of size 500 and above.

FlexiBowl® units of size 500 and above are fitted with lifting eyebolts, to be used to transport the machine from the packaging box to its final position.

Important

The eyebolts are fixed on swivel bases: when slinging the FlexiBowl® it is important to make sure that the load lies in the plane of the eyebolt’s eye and does not exceed 45° with respect to the vertical axis, a situation that could cause shear loads that would stress the eyebolts abnormally.

Warning

The handles on FlexiBowl® units of size 500 and above are NOT to be used for lifting, but only serve to guide placement on the machine surface.
