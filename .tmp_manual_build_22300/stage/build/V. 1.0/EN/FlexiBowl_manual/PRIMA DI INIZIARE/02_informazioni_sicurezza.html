Safety Information#

The following safety instructions, general precautions and regulations relating to handling and the operating environment must be scrupulously observed to ensure the safety of personnel, the integrity of the product and the correct operation of the system.

Warning

User responsibility

Compliance with all safety regulations set out in this section is mandatory and is the responsibility of the end user. Failure to comply may cause injury to persons, damage to equipment or compromise the operation of the system.

Operator safety area#

The zones around the machine are divided as follows:

Term

Description

Control zones

These are the zones in which the user and other operators can perform control and command operations of the machine’s cyclic functions (“operator station”), both in automatic and semi-automatic mode, using the appropriate control panels or for the execution of manual operations.

Maintenance/adjustment zones

These are the zones in which mechanical maintenance technicians can carry out maintenance or adjustment operations. These zones are considered hazardous and not accessible during normal machine operation. Operators must be fully aware of the safety warnings and the personal protective equipment to be worn.

Hazardous zones

All zones inside (or surrounding) the machine where residual risks exist that may cause injury to persons are considered hazardous. Access to these zones by anyone is strictly prohibited while the machine is in operation.

The hazards and risks present in these zones are protected, as far as possible, by guards (covers, panels) and safety devices (sensors, microswitches, photoelectric barriers) which, when activated, bring the machine to a complete stop. However, when the machine is in operation, it is strictly forbidden to work in hazardous zones as some risks may not have been completely eliminated.

Personal Protective Equipment (P.P.E.)#

When working near the FlexiBowl®, both for assembly operations and for maintenance and/or adjustment activities, general accident prevention regulations must be strictly observed. It is therefore important to use the personal protective equipment (P.P.E.) required for each individual operation. The complete list of personal protective equipment (P.P.E.) that may be required for the various procedures is provided below:

Symbol

Description

Mandatory use of protective or insulating gloves.

Indicates a requirement for personnel to wear protective or insulating gloves.

Mandatory use of protective eyewear.

Indicates a requirement for personnel to wear protective eyewear.

Mandatory use of safety footwear.

Indicates a requirement for personnel to wear safety footwear to protect the feet.

Mandatory use of noise protection devices.

Indicates a requirement for personnel to wear earmuffs or earplugs to protect hearing.

Mandatory use of protective clothing.

Indicates a requirement for personnel to wear specific protective clothing.

Mandatory use of a protective helmet.

Indicates a requirement for personnel to wear a helmet to protect the head.

Mandatory consultation of the manual/instruction booklet.

Indicates a requirement for personnel to consult (and understand) the operating and warning instructions for the machine before working with it.

The clothing worn by those operating or performing maintenance on the line must comply with the essential safety requirements defined by EU Reg. 2016/425 and the laws in force in the country where it is installed.

Safety devices#

In order to guarantee the complete safety of the operator and prevent access to the interior of the machine while it is in motion, the machine has been equipped with a series of safety devices which, when activated, bring it to a complete stop. The machine has been designed and equipped with safety systems to minimise risks to the operator. The machine is provided with the safety devices described in the following table. For the location of these devices, refer to the diagram below.

No.

Element

Description

1

Guard cover

Consists of fixed perimeter guards (covers), which serve to prevent access to the moving parts of the machine during the operating cycle and require specific tools for their removal.

2

Located on the control panel and allows the electrical power supply to be cut off in the event of:

  • risk to the safety of the operator;

  • electrical hazard on the machine;

  • mechanical or electrical work on the machine.

Warning

Due to the presence of pointed protrusions on surfaces or hard discs with Spikes, the operator may be exposed to the risk of abrasion and/or cuts in the event of contact with them. Wear appropriate P.P.E. when working near such surfaces or hard discs.

Warning

In an emergency, remove the power supply connected to the control panel to safely disable the FlexiBowl® controls.

Hazards and risks#

Noise#

Noise measurements were carried out in accordance with the provisions of standards UNI EN ISO 11200:2020 – “Noise emitted by machinery and equipment - Guidelines for the use of basic standards for the determination of emission sound pressure levels at a work station and at other specified positions” and UNI EN ISO 3746:2011 “Determination of sound power levels and sound energy levels of noise sources using sound pressure - Survey method using an enveloping measurement surface over a reflecting plane”. The assessment was also carried out following the procedures set out in DIRECTIVE 2006/42/EC point 1.5.8 – point 1.7.4.2 letter u.

The complete report is contained in the documentation relating to the partly completed machinery held by ARS. The measurement report is available on request to users and integrators.

Measurements were taken under 3 different operating conditions:

  • FlexiBowl in operation (MOVE, SHAKE, FLIP) without components present (“no-load operation”);

  • FlexiBowl in operation (MOVE, SHAKE, FLIP) with components present on the rotating disc, rigid plastic components;

  • FlexiBowl in operation (MOVE, SHAKE, FLIP) with components present on the rotating disc, metal components;

During no-load operating cycles, the A-weighted equivalent continuous sound pressure level at the workstation is 73.3 dB(A). For operating cycles with components, since the A-weighted emission sound pressure level at the workstations exceeds 80 dB(A), the A-weighted sound power level emitted by the machine is also reported in the following table.

Vibrations#

The vibrations produced by the machine, depending on the operating conditions, are not hazardous to the health of operators.

Warning

Excessive vibration can only be caused by a mechanical fault, which must be immediately reported and rectified to avoid compromising the safety of the line and the operators.

Electromagnetic compatibility#

The supplied machine contains electronic components subject to Electromagnetic Compatibility regulations, conditioned by conducted and radiated emissions.

Emission values comply with regulatory requirements through the use of components conforming to the Electromagnetic Compatibility directive, appropriate connections and the installation of filters where necessary. The machine therefore complies with the Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) directive.

Warning

Maintenance activities on the electrical equipment carried out in a non-compliant manner or incorrect component replacements may compromise the effectiveness of the adopted solutions.

Residual risks#

The machine has been designed to ensure the essential safety requirements for the operator. Safety has been integrated into the design and construction of the machine as far as possible; however, residual risks remain from which operators must be protected, particularly during:

  • transport and installation;

  • normal operation;

  • adjustment and fine-tuning;

  • maintenance and cleaning;

  • disassembly and dismantling.

For each residual risk, a description is provided below, along with the zone or part of the machine affected (unless the entire machine is involved) and procedural information on how to avoid it:

Risk Description and procedural information
HAZARDS ARISING FROM HANDLING

PICTOGRAMS:
Handling procedures are described in the "Transport and Installation" chapter of this instruction manual.
Residual risk:
The following operations:
  • unloading of packaging,
  • opening of packaging,
  • machine handling
expose operators to the risk of suspended loads and crushing. These operations must be carried out exclusively by personnel specialised in the use of lifting equipment and who have been appropriately trained for this purpose.
RISK OF ABRASION, CUTS, IMPACT

PICTOGRAMS:
Due to the presence of pointed protrusions on "Rotary Disc" surfaces with Spikes, the operator may be exposed to the risk of abrasion and/or cuts in the event of contact with them.
Wear appropriate P.P.E. when working near such "Rotary Disc" surfaces.
ELECTRICAL HAZARD

PICTOGRAMS:
Machine access and maintenance operations expose operators to electrical risk. Work on live equipment must be carried out exclusively by expert and qualified personnel.
The following safety measures are recommended:
  • pay close attention to safety pictograms relating to electrical risk;
  • do not carry out maintenance work without first isolating the electrical power supply;
  • consult the manuals of commercial equipment for any specific recommendations;
  • periodically inspect the equipotential protection circuit.
HAZARD ARISING FROM LIGHTING DISTURBANCE

PICTOGRAMS:
The "Backlight" is located inside the machine body, out of the operator's direct line of sight, and is almost completely shielded by the machine body guards.
Residual risk:
The operator may suffer eye damage if they observe the intense light of the "Backlight" for a prolonged period of time.
HAZARD ARISING FROM DUST, SWARF, ETC.

PICTOGRAMS:
At the end of the work cycle, residues from the fed parts or dust accumulations may remain on the machine's "Rotary Disc".
Carry out thorough cleaning of the "Rotary Disc" after each use, as described in chapter 7 of this manual.

Warning

Do not carry out maintenance or cleaning activities without first de-energising all energy sources present.

Warning

It is strictly forbidden to remove the safety guards installed on the machine or to open fixed guards without first isolating the electrical and pneumatic power supply to the machine.

It is the responsibility of the user to:

  • analyse the risks that may arise during a handling and installation phase within their own premises (the analyses carried out on machine handling have been performed solely in consideration of the machine’s own characteristics);

  • raise awareness and instruct personnel assigned to workstation operations and personnel responsible for operating the machine;

  • apply visual safety signage in the working environment after having assessed the risks within transit or control areas.

Integration with robotic systems#

Cell safety requirements#

Warning

The FlexiBowl® operates in close connection with third-party robotic systems. The user must ensure that the working area is equipped with all necessary safety measures required by applicable regulations.

Caution during operation#

Warning

During system operation, always take into account:

  • Physical footprint of the robot and the FlexiBowl

  • Trajectories and speeds of robotic movements

  • Possible unexpected situations (falling parts, picking errors)

  • Hazardous zones during FlexiBowl vibration phases

General precautions before intervention#

Disconnection of power supplies#

Warning

Before carrying out any maintenance, modification or inspection on the system, always ensure that:

  • All electrical power sources are disconnected (VisionController, FlexiBowl, Camera, Illuminator)

  • The pneumatic supply is discharged and disconnected (if present)

  • Connecting cables are physically unplugged

  • The robot is in safe mode or completely switched off

Safety procedures#

Warning

Do not rely solely on switches: use lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures where available.

Modifications and tampering#

Prohibition of unauthorised modifications#

Warning

Never modify the product or its components without the express written authorisation of ARS S.r.l.

Consequences of modifications#

Warning

Unauthorised modifications may:

  • Cause system malfunctions

  • Invalidate the warranty

  • Create risks of injury, electric shock or fire

  • Compromise the product’s safety certifications
