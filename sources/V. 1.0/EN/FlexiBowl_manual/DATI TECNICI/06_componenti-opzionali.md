# **Optional Components**

To make the FlexiBowl® suitable for every need, the operator can make use of a wide range of accessories designed to handle every type of component.

:::{note}
Accessories can be combined, but **not all combinations are possible**; for more information, refer to the accessories {ref}`overview and compatibility <compatibility>` page.
:::

## {ref}`Backlight <backlight>`

There are two types of “Backlight”: 

- Standard 
- Strobe 

The first is powered continuously from inside, and it can be switched on and off via software or digital I/O (chapter ............). The second is controlled externally via the “strobe” input of the input connector (chapter .........). It is the customer's responsibility to supply the correct 24Vdc power to switch it on. It is a light located under the glass window of the sliding plane, so that its light can hit the surface or rigid disc and the profile of the components above it becomes visible to the vision system. 

The “Backlight” is available with: 

- White light 
- Red light 
- Infrared light 

:::{note}
The infrared “Backlight” emits invisible light and may therefore appear not to work. Check with a camera fitted with an infrared filter to verify that it works. Most smartphones can display infrared. 
:::

## {ref}`Diverter <diverter>`

It is needed to move parts away from the edge of the FlexiBowl®. It is positioned before the “Flip” unit (pre-flip diverter) so that most of the parts are hit by the pulse, and before the vision window (post-flip diverter) to make picking them easier.

## {ref}`Brush Diverter <brush>`

It consists of: 

- Central brush: its function is to move the components away from the central flange, so as to prevent them from accumulating around it. 
- Diverter brush: has the same function as the steel diverter. It is used when parts tend to jam between the “Rotary Disc” and the metal diverter; this situation mainly occurs when using “Rotary Discs” with Spikes. 
- Adjustable brush: its function is to untangle and “lay flat” components that tend to tangle together or to stand upright during normal operation of the FlexiBowl. 

## {ref}`Wedge Diverter <wedge>`

The Wedge Diverter option is compatible with sizes 1200/800/650/500 in the standard version (C-CC); it is not compatible with any Edge version (200E, 350E and 500E). This option is intended to move components away from the outer edge of the FlexiBowl and is particularly suitable for parts that roll or slide easily. 

## {ref}`Emptying <emptying>`

The “Quick Emptying” option is used to evacuate the residual parts from the FlexiBowl® before a production changeover. The “Quick Emptying” option can be activated via digital I/O or via software. When the “Quick Emptying” command is sent, a portion of the ring lowers to let the residual parts out. Sensors indicate whether the “Emptying Door” is open or closed. The sensor status can also be read on the input connector (Chapter .......). The “Quick Emptying” sequence consists of a preconfigured sequence of combined clockwise and counterclockwise movements. In the “Quick Emptying” sequence only the movement speed can be changed. The number of movements and their direction cannot be changed.
