# **Electrical Technical Data**

## Power Supply Data

The following are the nominal electrical supply parameters applicable to all variants of the FlexiBowl® FB 3.0 family.

### General Parameters

- **Nominal voltage**: 230 / 120 Vac

- **Nominal frequency**: 50 Hz / 60 Hz

### Nominal Absorbed Current

The absorbed current varies depending on the installed model:

:header-rows: 1

  - 1.50 A

* - FlexiBowl® 650
  - 2.00 A
* - FlexiBowl® 800

## Electrical Protections

### Fuses

The protection circuit is implemented using fast-acting fuses. The nominal values per model are as follows:

:::{list-table}
:widths: 50 50
:header-rows: 1
* - Device
  - Fuse
* - FlexiBowl® 200
  - 3 A
* - FlexiBowl® 350
  - 3 A
* - FlexiBowl® 500
  - 3 A
* - FlexiBowl® 650
  - 3 A
* - FlexiBowl® 800
  - 4 A
* - FlexiBowl® 1200
  - 5 A
:::

### Electrical Protection Class

- **Protection class**: Class I

Protection is achieved through basic insulation and connection of all metal parts to the protective conductor (PE – system earth).

### Earthing

The device must be connected to the system earth circuit before any other operation.

:::{attention}
Before switching on the FlexiBowl, verify that the device is correctly earthed to the main system.
:::

## General Notes and Warnings

:::{attention}
All connection operations must be carried out with the device switched off and disconnected from the power supply.
:::

:::{attention}
The earth connection is mandatory and must be verified before every power-on.
:::

:::{attention}
In the event of an anomaly indicated by the red LED (Ready/Fault), refer to the fault table specific to the model.
:::
