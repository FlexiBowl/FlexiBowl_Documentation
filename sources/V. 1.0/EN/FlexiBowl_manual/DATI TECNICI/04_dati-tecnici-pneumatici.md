:::{important}
For its pneumatic supply, the FlexiBowl® requires clean compressed air in accordance with **ISO 8573-1:2010 class 1.3.2**, that is:

* Solid particles must be ≤0.1μm in size;

* Pressure dew point ≤-20°C (no condensation or frost);

* Oil concentration (including aerosols and vapors) ≤0.1 mg/m³.
:::

:::{note}
*The minimum supply pressure for the FlexiBowl® 1200 becomes 3 bar if it is equipped with emptying.
:::

:::{note}
**Average consumption measured with the use of flip only. Installing the blows may increase consumption by up to 75%.
:::

:::{warning}
Using non-compliant air can cause malfunctions, reduced performance and premature wear of the pneumatic components.
:::

An optional **{ref}`external air filtration kit <airfilter>`** is available on request. The kit is recommended where the quality of the compressed air in the customer's plant is not known or not guaranteed, and it makes it possible to reach the required quality class.
