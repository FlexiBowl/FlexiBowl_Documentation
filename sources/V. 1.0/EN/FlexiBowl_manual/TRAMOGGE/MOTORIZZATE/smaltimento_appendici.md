# **Disposal and Annexes**

This section contains suggestions and instructions for correctly carrying out the operations of decommissioning, dismantling and removal of the partly completed machine at the end of its operating life.

:::{warning}
The following operations are the responsibility of authorized personnel.
:::

## Dismantling

- Make sure there is enough space around the partly completed machine to allow operators all the necessary movements without risk.
- Switch off the general electrical power supply of the partly completed machine (the Customer's responsibility) and lock it in the OFF position.
- Disassemble the partly completed machine, proceeding for each group from top to bottom.

:::{warning}
Pay particular attention to the possibility of parts or components of the partly completed machine falling during removal: this could cause serious danger to operators.
:::

- Remove the moving parts and, as far as possible, separate the various components by type of material (plastic, metal, etc.), so that they can be disposed of through separate collection.
- Remove the parts of the partly completed machine from the work area and take them away, taking all necessary precautions.
- Before lifting components of considerable size and/or weight, check that the lifting devices are correctly secured and use only suitable slings and equipment.
- The partly completed machine is built from various materials, recyclable and not: removal therefore requires proper separation of the materials (steel, aluminium, copper, bronze, special alloys, plastic, etc.).
- The Manufacturer accepts no liability for damage resulting from any use of the individual components other than that prescribed.

:::{note}
Dismantling must comply with the applicable laws. In case of difficulties in the dismantling and disposal operations, or for greater safety, contact the Manufacturer, stating the reason for the removal and the serial number of the partly completed machine.
:::

## Storage

If the partly completed machine is not used for a long period (for example more than a month), it is recommended to:
- switch off the partly completed machine;
- disconnect the electrical power supply;
- clean all visible parts with a jet of air;
- open any motor guards and clean with a jet of air;
- cover with a thin layer of lubricating oil all the parts that could rust (rollers, chains, guides, etc.);
- cover the entire partly completed machine with a waterproof sheet;
- store the partly completed machine in a dry environment. To avoid damage to the electrical parts, make sure the temperature is always between **4 °C and 40 °C** and that there is no risk of condensation or rapid corrosion (caused for example by salty air).
