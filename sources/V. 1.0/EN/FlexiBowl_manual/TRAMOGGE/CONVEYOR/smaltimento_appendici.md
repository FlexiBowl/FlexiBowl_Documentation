# **Decommissioning, Disposal and Annexes**

:::{warning}
Decommissioning and dismantling operations must be entrusted to personnel specialized in such activities. In particular, only the person in charge of end-of-life dismantling and disposal may:
- disconnect the parts mechanically and electrically following the disassembly instructions and drawings;
- transport the parts from the workplace to the disposal centre for component sorting.
:::

:::{warning}
The machine does not contain hazardous components or substances that require special removal procedures.
:::

## Decommissioning

| Phase | Action |
|:--- |:--- |
| 1 | Disconnect the machine's power sources. |
| 2 | Store the machine in a covered, dry place, protected from the weather. |
| 3 | Cover the machine with a breathable sheet. |

## Disposal

Before scrapping, it is mandatory to remove and dispose of, in compliance with the applicable local laws and regulations, all parts that may harm the environment.

| Material | Action |
|:--- |:--- |
| **Plastic** | Must be dismantled and disposed of separately. |
| **Lubricants and oils** | Must be collected and delivered to specialized and authorized collection and disposal centres. |
| **Iron** | Dismantle and collect separately. Must be recycled through the appropriate collection centres. |
| **Steel** | Dismantle and collect separately. Must be recycled through the appropriate collection centres. |
| **Aluminium** | Dismantle and collect separately. Must be recycled through the appropriate collection centres. |
| **Pneumatic or electrical components** | Must be dismantled for reuse if still in good condition or, where possible, overhauled and recycled. |

To dispose of the machine, proceed as follows:

| Phase | Action |
|:--- |:--- |
| 1 | Prepare a large work area free of obstructions, on a smooth, level floor, to carry out the dismantling operations safely. |
| 2 | Disconnect the machine from the mains, detaching the power cables and all electrical components after taking the safety measures required for such work. |
| 3 | Proceed with disassembly, separating the machine components into the groups shown in the previous table for separate disposal. |

:::{warning}
For disassembly of the commercial parts or custom-order material that are part of the machine supplied by the Manufacturer, refer to the relevant suppliers' manuals.
:::

:::{note}
Pursuant to the WEEE Directive 2012/19/EU, if the purchased component/appliance bears the crossed-out wheelie bin symbol, at the end of its life the product must be collected separately from other waste.
:::

:::{warning}
It is mandatory to comply with the laws in force on disposal in the country where the machine is installed.
:::
