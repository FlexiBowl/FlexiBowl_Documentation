# [MEC] **Decommissioning and Disposal**

:::{attention}
Decommissioning and dismantling operations must be entrusted exclusively to specialized and authorized personnel. 
:::

In particular, only the personnel in charge of the dismantling and end-of-life disposal phase may carry out:
* **Disconnection:** Mechanical, electrical and pneumatic disconnection of the components according to the disassembly instructions and the design diagrams.
* **Logistics and handling:** Transport of the parts from the installation position to the collection/disposal center for the subsequent separation of the materials.

### Inventory of the machine materials

To facilitate recycling and separate collection, the main materials that make up the machine are listed below:

| Macro-category | Specific material type |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Metal components** | Painted, plastic-coated or galvanized ferritic steel.<br>300 / 400 series stainless steel. |
| **Polymers and elastomers** | Polyethylene plastic material.<br>Elastomers, PTFE, graphite. |
| **Fluids and lubricants** | Gear oil.<br>Grease for industrial lubrication. |
| **Electrical / electronic components** | Electric motors.<br>Electric cables with their insulating sheaths.<br>Electronic control devices, boards and actuators. |

:::{attention}
The machine does not contain intrinsically toxic or harmful components or substances that require extraordinary or special neutralization procedures before dismantling.
:::

## Disposal and Demolition

The final demolition of the machine must be entrusted to qualified personnel, each for their own area of technical competence (mechanical, electrical, waste management). When the machine is to be sent for disposal, it must first be made completely safe.

:::{attention}
Before starting any structural disassembly operation, verify visually and with instruments the complete disconnection of the machine from the electrical power supply and the pneumatic power supply.
:::

:::{attention}
For the disassembly of commercial parts or sub-supplied material integrated in the machine supplied by ARS S.r.l., refer to the technical specifications contained in the relevant manual of the original manufacturer.
:::

### Management of waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE)

:::{info} 
**WEEE Directive 2012/19/EU:** If the component or equipment purchased is marked with the crossed-out wheeled bin symbol, it means that the product, at the end of its useful life, must not be treated as municipal waste but must be delivered to a specialized separate collection center.
:::

#### Local regulatory obligations

:::{attention}
The Customer/User is required to strictly observe and comply with the laws and decrees in force on disposal and environmental protection in the specific country in which the machine is installed.
:::
