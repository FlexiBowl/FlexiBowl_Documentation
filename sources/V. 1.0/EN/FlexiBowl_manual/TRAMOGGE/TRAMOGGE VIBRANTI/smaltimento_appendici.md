# **Decommissioning and Disposal**

:::{attention}
Decommissioning and dismantling operations must be entrusted exclusively to specialised and authorised personnel.
:::

In particular, only the personnel in charge of the dismantling phase and of end-of-life disposal may carry out:
* **Disconnection:** Mechanical, electrical and pneumatic disconnection of the components according to the disassembly instructions and the design diagrams.
* **Logistics and handling:** Transport of the parts from the installation site to the collection/disposal centre for the subsequent separation of the materials.

### Inventory of the machine materials

To facilitate recycling and separate collection, the main materials that make up the machine are listed below:

| Main category | Specific type of material |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Metal components** | Painted, plastic-coated or galvanised ferritic steel.<br>300 / 400 series stainless steel. |
| **Polymers and elastomers** | Polyethylene plastic.<br>Elastomers, PTFE, graphite. |
| **Fluids and lubricants** | Gear oil.<br>Industrial lubrication grease. |
| **Electrical / electronic components** | Electric motors.<br>Electric cables with their insulating sheaths.<br>Electronic control devices, boards and actuators. |

:::{attention}
The machine does not contain any intrinsically toxic or harmful components or substances that would require extraordinary or special neutralisation procedures before dismantling.
:::

## Disposal and Demolition

The final demolition of the machine must be entrusted to qualified personnel, each within their own area of technical competence (mechanical, electrical, waste management). When the machine is to be sent for disposal, it must first be made completely safe.

:::{attention}
Before starting any structural disassembly operation, verify visually and with instruments the complete disconnection of the machine from the electrical power supply and from the pneumatic power supply.
:::

:::{attention}
For the disassembly of off-the-shelf parts or sub-supplied material integrated into the machine supplied by ARS S.r.l., refer to the technical specifications contained in the relevant manual of the original manufacturer.
:::

### Management of waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE)

:::{info} 
**WEEE Directive 2012/19/EU:** If the purchased component or equipment is marked with the crossed-out bin symbol, this means that the product, at the end of its useful life, must not be treated as municipal waste but must be taken to a specialised separate collection centre.
:::

#### Local regulatory obligations

:::{attention}
The Customer/User is required to strictly observe and comply with the laws and decrees in force on disposal and environmental protection in the specific country where the machine is installed.
:::
