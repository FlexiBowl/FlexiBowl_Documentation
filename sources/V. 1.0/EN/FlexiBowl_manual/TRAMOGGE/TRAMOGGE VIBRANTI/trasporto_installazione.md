# **Transport and Installation**

:::{important}
Lifting and handling operations must be carried out exclusively by specialised and trained personnel qualified to perform such activities.
:::

<p style="font-size: 1.05em; color: #2c3e50; line-height: 1.6; padding: 5px 0;">
  The machine has been designed so that a <strong>forklift of adequate capacity</strong> is required during the packing, transport and assembly phases. Note that the machine has no dedicated lifting points (for example: eyebolts).

## Packaging 

The machine is shipped by ARS s.r.l. from the production plant to the plant of the Customer who uses it.

* **Standard shipping methods:**
* **Normal protective packaging:** Suitable for short and medium distances.
* **Special protective packaging:** Suitable for long distances.

:::{note} 
**Note on transport:** Shipment must be carried out with covered or tarpaulin-covered means of transport, depending on the type of load.
:::

### Permitted environmental conditions 

The environment in which the machine is installed and used must be **indoors and sheltered from atmospheric agents** (rain, hail, snow, fog), airborne or combustible dust, corrosive vapours or sources of excessive heat. The area **must not be classified as ATEX**.

In particular, the installation and use environment must not present:
* Exposure to corrosive fumes or oily vapours;
* Exposure to excessive humidity (above 85 %) and rapid changes in relative humidity (above 0.005 p.u./h);
* Exposure to excessive or abrasive dust;
* Exposure to explosive dust or gas mixtures or to salt-laden air;
* Exposure to abnormal vibrations, impacts or shocks;
* Exposure to high or rapid temperature changes (above 5K/h) or nuclear radiation.

**Environmental operating limits:**

| Permitted environmental conditions | Operating values |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ambient temperature** | 5 ÷ 40 °C |
| **Humidity range** | 5 - 90 % (non-condensing) |
| **Ambient lighting** | Neon light |

:::{attention}
Environmental conditions other than those specified may cause serious damage to the machine. Placing the machine in environments that do not correspond to those indicated voids the warranty for the parts to be replaced.
:::

:::{important}
The work surface must be sufficiently lit. If shadow areas or differences in level are found at the workplace, it is the user's responsibility to provide suitable lighting devices. If these requirements are not complied with, the Manufacturer declines all liability.
:::

### Installation Area 

:::{attention}
Never lift the unit by the moving assembly, as the vibrator has been calibrated at the factory according to your specific requirements and could go out of calibration.
:::

The unit must not be installed where the channel could come into contact with rigid objects or adjacent surfaces; maintain a **clearance of about 3-5 cm** between the vibrating part and the surrounding static parts.

Bulk Feeders can withstand a specific maximum load. It is essential to size the structural support so that it safely sustains the total weight of the system under vibrating load:

| Model | Maximum permissible load | Output (t/h) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1.5 l** | 1 kg | 0.6 |
| **3 l**   | 1.5 kg | 0.6 |
| **5 l**   | 6 kg | 2 |
| **10 l**  | 6 kg | 2 |
| **20 l**  | 6 kg | 2 |
| **40 l**  | 15 kg | 5 |

The control unit must be installed as close as possible to the Bulk Feeder, in a dry, clean and vibration-free place.

### Electrical Connection 

:::{attention}
Before carrying out any electrical connection operation, it is important to check that the machine is switched off.
:::

:::{attention}
Make sure the customer's electrical supply line has been isolated beforehand.
:::

The compliance of the connection between the machine and the earthing system is entirely the responsibility and burden of the purchaser.

:::{attention}
The operation must be carried out exclusively by specialised and authorised personnel (electrical maintenance technician).
:::

Before proceeding with the electrical connection, check that:
* The technician is aware of the regulations in force in the country of installation;
* The frequency and supply voltage values correspond to those of the local network;
* The cross-section of the electrical cables used is adequate for the current draw and maximum loads of the machine;
* The earthing of the circuit is fully compliant with the **EN 60204-1** standards;
* The materials used in the earthing system have adequate strength or mechanical protection.

:::{attention}
Do not work with wet hands or objects. In the event of fire, do not use water on electrical components.
:::
