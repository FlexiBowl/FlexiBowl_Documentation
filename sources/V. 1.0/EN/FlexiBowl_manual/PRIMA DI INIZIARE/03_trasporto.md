# **Transport and Handling**

## Packaging

The machine is shipped by ARS s.r.l. from the production facility to the customer's premises. Depending on the transport distance, the customer's specific requirements, and the length of time the load remains in the packaging, the machine is shipped in the following ways:

- standard protective packaging for short and medium distances;
- special protective packaging for long distances.

Shipment must be carried out using covered or tarpaulined transport vehicles depending on the type of load.

### **Inspection upon arrival**

```{note}
Upon receipt of the product, before signing the delivery note:

1. **External inspection of the packaging**: Check the integrity of the box and outer packaging. Look for any signs of impacts, crushing or moisture damage.

2. **Content verification**: Compare the contents with the delivery note. Verify that all ordered components are present.
```

### **In case of damage or discrepancies**

```{note}
If any issues are found:

- Do NOT sign the receipt as "conforming"
- Note any damage on the transport document
- Photograph any visible damage
- Contact ARS customer service immediately:
    [service@arsautomation.com](mailto:service@arsautomation.com)
    [us.service@arsautomation.com](mailto:us.service@arsautomation.com) if contacting from America
```

## Transport and handling of the packaging

:::{tip}
**During transport:**
- Always handle the packaging in an upright position (observe the "THIS WAY UP" arrows on the packaging)
- Do not drop or knock the package
- Use trolleys or pallet trucks suitable for the weight
- Avoid sudden temperature changes

To handle the machine body in its packaging, proceed as described:

::::{list-table}
:widths: 15 60
:header-rows: 1

* - Step
  - Operation

* - 1
  - Position the forklift forks under the wooden crate in which the machine is stored.

* - 2
  - Ensure that the forks protrude from the front of the load (by at least 5 cm), for a length sufficient to eliminate any risk of the transported load tipping over.

* - 3
  - Raise the forks until they make contact with the load.
    :::{note}
    If necessary, secure the load to the forks with clamps or similar devices.
    :::

* - 4
  - Slowly raise the load by a few tens of centimetres and check its stability, ensuring that the centre of gravity of the load is positioned at the centre of the lifting forks.

* - 5
  - Tilt the mast backwards (towards the driver's position) to increase the tipping moment and ensure greater load stability during transport.

* - 6
  - Adjust the transport speed according to the floor surface and the type of load, avoiding sudden manoeuvres.

### Storage

:::{tip}
**During storage:**
- Store in a dry, covered location
- Do not stack other loads on top of the packaging
- Do not climb on or lean against the packaging
- Observe the environmental conditions indicated in the previous table
:::

### Unpacking

:::{tip}
**During unpacking:**
- Open carefully to avoid damaging internal components
- Keep the original packaging for any returns or future transport
- Verify that all accessories and documentation are present
:::

To **remove the packaging**, proceed as described:

:::{list-table}
:widths: 15 60
:header-rows: 1

* - Step
  - Operation

* - 1
  - Position the FlexiBowl® in its designated location.

* - 2
  - Unscrew the FlexiBowl® from the base of the wooden crate used for shipping.

* - 3
  - Remove the USB device and the test report from the crate. Keep it for future use.

* - 4
  - Use the side handles on the FlexiBowl® to lift it and remove it from the crate.

:::

:::{warning}
The manual lifting operation of the FlexiBowl® from the wooden crate requires the presence of 2 operators.
:::

For handling the machine and/or its parts, refer to the section {ref}`"FlexiBowl® Transport" <transflex>`.

### Packaging disposal

The packaging is an integral part of the supply and is not taken back; therefore, its disposal is the responsibility of the purchaser.
Any disposal or destruction must be carried out in compliance with the regulations in force in the user's country, taking into account the nature of the materials:
- timber for the crates;
- plastic film for machine protection and adhesive tape for securing it;
- bags of moisture-absorbing material;
- etc.

## FlexiBowl® Transport

**ARS s.r.l.** uses appropriate packaging and fixings depending on the transport method, to ensure the integrity and preservation of the machine during transport.

Upon receipt of the machine, verify that no part has been damaged during transport and/or handling.
**If any damage is found, it must be reported immediately to the Manufacturer.**

The handling activities described in this section must be carried out by personnel qualified for such operations: personnel specifically trained to safely perform loading, unloading and handling operations using lifting equipment, and who are familiar with accident prevention rules.

ARS S.r.l. accepts no liability for damage to property or persons caused by accidents resulting from failure to follow the instructions contained in this manual.

:::{warning}
Standard **EN ISO 11228-1-2022** prohibits the manual lifting of loads exceeding 25 kg (the limit may be lower depending on the operator's sex and age); therefore, for safety reasons, the use of lifting machinery is mandatory for FlexiBowl® units exceeding this limit. It should also be noted that the weight table shown above, and in other chapters of this manual, refers to FlexiBowl® units **without** accessories fitted; the weight may increase depending on the purchased configuration, in which case the weight will be indicated accordingly. FlexiBowl® units exceeding the 25 kg limit are those of size 500 and above.
:::

FlexiBowl® units of size 500 and above are fitted with lifting eyebolts, to be used for transporting the machine from the packaging crate to its final position.

::::{important}
The eyebolts are fixed on swivelling bases: when slinging the FlexiBowl®, it is important to ensure that the load lies in the plane of the eyebolt ring and does not exceed 45° relative to the vertical axis, as this could generate shear loads that would stress the eyebolts abnormally.
