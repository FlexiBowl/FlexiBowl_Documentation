# **Preliminary Information**

This section contains legal information and important warnings concerning the use of the FlexiBowl® and of this documentation.   Please read carefully before proceeding with the installation and use of the system.

---

## Recipients and target audience

```{note}
**Who this manual is for**

This documentation is addressed to qualified technicians with skills in:
- Integration of robotic systems
- Configuration of industrial vision systems
- Installation and maintenance of electromechanical equipment

The reader is assumed to have the basic technical knowledge necessary to understand the information provided.   Information that can easily be deduced from technical drawings or diagrams may not be detailed further.
```

---

## Important warnings

### **Read before use**
```{warning}

Before using the FlexiBowl®, it is mandatory to:
- Read this manual in full to ensure correct use of the system
- Comply with the operating instructions and recommendations
- Adequately train the personnel in charge of use
- Consult the instruction manuals of all connected hardware components (FlexiVision One, Bulk Feeder, VisionController, Camera, Robot, etc.)

Failure to comply with these instructions may cause malfunctions, damage to equipment or dangerous situations.
```
### **Operating context and limitations of liability**

The FlexiBowl® is a flexible feeding system with a vibrating rotating disc for the random positioning and orientation of components for the purpose of robotic picking.

```{warning}
During use, the operator must:
- Take into account the physical dimensions of the system
- Monitor the movements of the robot and of the feeder
- Anticipate and manage unexpected operating situations
- Comply with the safety standards applicable to industrial robots and machinery
```
```{warning}
**ARS S.r.l. declines all liability for injury to persons or damage to property resulting from the movement of machines and systems connected to the FlexiVision One software.**

The integration of the system into the work environment and the risk assessment are the responsibility of the system integrator and the end user.
```

## Operators

To establish with certainty the skills and qualifications of the operators assigned to the various tasks (start-up, cleaning, routine maintenance), consult the following table:

:::{list-table}
:header-rows: 1
:widths: 30 70

* - Qualification
  - Definition

* - **System integrator**
  - Personnel in charge of layout design, component sizing and verification of the technical requirements for the installation of the FlexiBowl®.

* - **Installation technician**
  - Personnel in charge of mechanical assembly, electrical and pneumatic connection and network configuration.

* - **Operator**
  - User personnel trained and authorized to use and run the machine for production purposes, for the activities for which it was built and supplied. They must be able to carry out all the operations necessary for the proper functioning of the machine and for the safety of themselves and any colleagues. They must have proven experience in the correct use of this type of machine and be trained, informed and instructed in this regard. In case of doubt, they must report any anomaly to their superior.

    **Note:** Not authorized to carry out any maintenance activity.
    
* - **Mechanical maintenance technician**
  - Qualified technician able to:

    * carry out preventive/corrective maintenance on all mechanical parts of the machine subject to maintenance or repair;
    * have access to all parts of the machine for visual analysis, checking the condition of the equipment, adjustments and calibrations;
    * work on the mechanical components for adjustments, maintenance and repairs;
    * read pneumatic and hydraulic diagrams, technical drawings and spare parts lists.

    In exceptional cases, they are authorized to run the machine with reduced safety. Where necessary, they may give the operator instructions for the proper use of the machine for production purposes.

    **Note:** Not authorized to work on live electrical systems (if any).

* - **Electrical maintenance technician**
  - Qualified technician able to:
    * carry out preventive/corrective maintenance on all mechanical parts of the machine subject to maintenance or repair;
    * have access to all parts of the machine for visual analysis, checking the condition of the equipment, adjustments and calibrations;
    * run the machine like the operator;
    * work on the adjustments and on the electrical systems for maintenance, repair and replacement of worn parts;
    * read electrical diagrams and verify the correct functional cycle. Where necessary, they may give the operator instructions for the proper use of the machine for production purposes. They may work with voltage present inside electrical cabinets, junction boxes, control equipment, etc. only if they are a suitable person (PEI). (Refer to standard **EN50110-1**). They do not carry out software programming of systems such as PLCs (logic or safety), and cannot change system passwords.

* - **Software specialist technician**
  - Qualified technician able to:
    * carry out preventive/corrective activities on all software parts of the machine;
    * have access to all parts of the machine for visual analysis, checking the condition of the equipment, adjustments and calibrations. Qualified technician of the Manufacturer with proven experience and training in systems based on: PLC/PC, drives, etc. (knowledge of programming, machine functions, etc.) for complex operations such as, for example:
    * modification of machine data;
    * creation of work programs;
    * adjustment of drive parameters, etc., being familiar with the production, technological and construction cycle of the machine supplied. They may work inside electrical cabinets, junction boxes, control equipment, etc. with voltage present only if they are a suitable person (PEI) (Refer to standard **EN50110-1**). Their skills are electronic and/or software.

* - **Manufacturer's technician**
  - Technician qualified by the Manufacturer and/or its distributor for complex operations, being familiar with the production cycle for building the machine. This person acts in accordance with the user's requests. Their skills are mechanical.

* - **Trained person**
  - Groups together all the qualifications listed in this table: this is someone who has been informed, instructed and trained on the work and on any hazards arising from improper use. They also know the importance of the safety devices, the accident-prevention regulations and the conditions for working safely.

:::
