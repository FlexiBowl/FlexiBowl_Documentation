# **Glossary**

```{list-table}
:header-rows: 1
:widths: 25 75

* - Term
  - Definition
* - **ATEX**
  - From the French *ATmosphères EXplosibles*: the conventional name of the two European directives governing equipment intended for explosive atmospheres (2014/34/EU) and the safety and health of workers exposed to such atmospheres (99/92/EC). The machines described in the manual are not intended for ATEX-classified environments.
* - **Backlight / Toplight**
  - Lighting devices of the vision system. The **Backlight** is placed under the window of the sliding surface and makes the silhouette of the components visible; the **Toplight** is mounted at the height of the camera and lights the vision area from above, highlighting the features of the components. Both are available with white, red or infrared light.
* - **Blow**
  - Jet of pressurized air that acts on the components to sort them and move them away from the limits of the vision area. There can be several types, according to the needs of the operator.
* - **Bulk Feeder**
  - Industrial storage and feeding device that holds the components and doses them continuously and uniformly towards the FlexiBowl®. It can be vibrating, belt-type (Conveyor Hopper) or motorized (Motorized Hopper).
* - **Chains, ropes or belts**
  - Elements designed and built for lifting purposes as an integral part of lifting machines or lifting accessories.
* - **Circular Tracking**
  - Option and operating mode that allows the FlexiBowl® to rotate continuously, without stopping at every cycle. An encoder tracks the rotation of the disc between the image capture and the pick, and the robot compensates for the movement; the picking area is shifted downstream with respect to the vision area. The term **Flexitracking** (or FlexiTrack), used in previous versions of the manual and in some interfaces, refers to the same option.
* - **Conveyor Hopper**
  - 60-litre hopper with motorized conveyor belt (model CH60), which receives bulk components from an upstream system and transfers them to a downstream system. It is partly completed machinery.
* - **Custom Disc**
  - Rigid disc designed to measure for a specific application, available for sizes 200, 350 and 500, which makes it possible to simplify the plant and reduce the need to reorient parts externally.
* - **D.P.I.**
  - The term Personal Protective Equipment (abbreviated PPE) means products whose function is to safeguard the worker (operator, maintenance staff, technician, etc.) who wears them or otherwise carries them, from risks to health and safety.
* - **Daisy chain**
  - Cascade connection of several devices on the same communication line. On the vibrating Bulk Feeders, the second RS485 port of the controller allows several Bulk Feeders to be connected in daisy chain.
* - **Damage**
  - Any negative consequence arising from the occurrence of the hazardous event.
* - **Declaration of incorporation**
  - Document accompanying partly completed machinery, certifying compliance with the essential safety requirements of the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC (Annex II, part 1, section B). The partly completed machinery must not be put into service until the final machinery into which it is incorporated has been declared compliant.
* - **Diverter**
  - Element that changes the position of the parts along the radius of the FlexiBowl®, to make the flip more effective and keep them away from critical areas. The types are the **Standard Diverter** (shaped polyethylene blocks, fitted by default), the **Brush Diverter** (assembly of up to 4 brushes) and the **Wedge Diverter** (a bump placed upstream of the flip area).
* - **Enable Output Port**
  - 4-pin connector of the vibrating Bulk Feeder controller used to enable the controller output by means of a PNP signal, when the Bulk Feeder is driven by an external device.
* - **Encoder**
  - Sensor that detects the rotation angle of the disc. In Circular Tracking its signal is sent both to the motor driver and to the robot controller.
* - **Failure**
  - A fault of any kind that prevents the normal operation of a machine, a plant, etc.
* - **Fixed guard / Movable guard**
  - The **fixed guard** is held in place permanently or by fastening systems (screws, bolts) that prevent its removal without tools. The **movable guard** is mechanically connected to the structure (for example with hinges or guides) and can be opened without tools.
* - **Flip**
  - Combined rotation and vibration movement of the FlexiBowl® that shuffles the components and promotes the correct presentation of new parts.
* - **Guard**
  - Physical barrier, designed as part of the machine, to provide protection.
* - **Hazard**
  - Potential source of harm which, if not avoided, entails a risk to the safety and health of exposed persons.
* - **Hopper**
  - Container from which the material is fed; in this manual it refers to the Bulk Feeders (vibrating, Conveyor Hopper or Motorized Hopper).
* - **Intended use**
  - Use of a machine in accordance with the information provided in the instructions for use.
* - **Lifting accessories**
  - Components or equipment not attached to lifting machines, which allow the load to be gripped, placed between the machine and the load or on the load itself, or intended to become an integral part of the load and to be placed on the market separately. Slings and their components are also considered lifting accessories.
* - **Machine**
  - Assembly equipped, or intended to be equipped, with a drive system, consisting of parts or components, at least one of which is movable, joined together rigidly for a specific application.
* - **Malfunction**
  - Faulty or inadequate operation of a machine or of one of its elements in performing a given function.
* - **Mix Mode**
  - Operating mode that allows parts of different types to be fed simultaneously onto the same surface of the FlexiBowl®, suited to sorting applications. The cycle consists of Detect, Move-Flip and Pick-Drop. Available for FlexiBowl® 500, 650 and 800.
* - **Modbus RTU**
  - Serial communication protocol over an RS485 line. On the vibrating Bulk Feeders it connects the controller to the FlexiBowl®.
* - **Motorized Hopper**
  - Paddle-belt elevator, available in 50, 100 and 150 litre sizes (TRM/MOT models), which lifts the material from the bottom to the top. It is partly completed machinery.
* - **Partly completed machinery**
  - An assembly which is almost machinery but which cannot in itself perform a specific application. It is intended exclusively to be incorporated into or assembled with other machinery, partly completed machinery or equipment to constitute machinery governed by the Machinery Directive. Conveyor Hopper and Motorized Hopper are partly completed machinery.
* - **Protection**
  - Defence against whatever could cause harm. An element placed between the person who may suffer harm and the cause of that harm, for hazards that cannot reasonably be eliminated or risks that cannot be sufficiently reduced during design. We distinguish:
    * **Active protection**: must be activated by the operators themselves (emergency stops, for example) and/or worn (PPE)
    * **Passive protection**: acts even without human command
* - **Quick Emptying**
  - System that allows a portion of the containment ring of the FlexiBowl® to be lowered to evacuate the residual components before a production changeover. It can be activated by digital I/O or by software.
* - **Reasonably foreseeable misuse**
  - Use of a machine in a way not intended by the designer, but which may result from readily predictable human behaviour.
* - **Residual risk**
  - Risk that remains after protective and preventive measures have been taken.
* - **Rigid disc**
  - Surface of the FlexiBowl® onto which the components are fed, made of rigid plastic. The available surfaces are described in the Surfaces and Rigid Discs pages.
* - **Risk**
  - Combination of the probability and the severity of an injury or damage to health that may arise in a hazardous situation.
* - **Sorting**
  - Application in which the robot selects and picks parts of different types present on the same working surface, according to a sorting logic.
* - **Standard Mode**
  - Default operating configuration of the FlexiBowl®, suitable for all applications. Robot and vision system share the same working area and operate in sequence (Detect, Pick-Drop, Move-Flip), while the hopper discharge takes place in parallel with the movement of the FlexiBowl®.
* - **Trained person**
  - A person who has been informed, instructed and trained on the job and on the hazards deriving from improper use, and who knows the importance of safety devices, accident prevention regulations and safe working conditions.
```
