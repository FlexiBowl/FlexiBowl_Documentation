#  **Glossary**

```{list-table}
:header-rows: 1
:widths: 25 75

* - Term
  - Definition
* - **Lifting accessories**
  - Components or equipment not attached to lifting machinery, which enable the load to be gripped, 
  placed between the machine and the load or on the load itself, or intended to become an integral 
  part of the load and to be placed on the market separately. 
  Slings and their components are also considered lifting accessories.
* - **Breakdown**
  - A fault of any kind that prevents the normal operation of a machine, a system, etc.
* - **Chains, ropes or belts**
  - Elements designed and manufactured for lifting purposes as an integral part of lifting machinery 
  or lifting accessories.
* - **Damage**
  - Any negative consequence arising from the occurrence of a hazardous event.
* - **P.P.E.**
  - The term Personal Protective Equipment (acronym PPE) refers to products whose function is to 
  safeguard the worker (operator, maintenance technician, technician, etc.) who wears or otherwise 
  carries them, from risks to health and safety.
* - **Machine**
  - An assembly equipped or intended to be equipped with a drive system, consisting of parts or 
  components, at least one of which is mobile, joined together for a specific application.
* - **Malfunction**
  - Defective or inadequate operation of a machine or one of its elements in performing a given function.
* - **Hazard**
  - A potential source of harm which, if not avoided, poses a risk to the safety and health of exposed persons.
* - **Protection**
  - A defence against what could cause harm. An element interposed between those who may suffer 
  harm and what may cause it, for hazards that cannot reasonably be eliminated or for risks that 
  cannot be sufficiently reduced during the design phase. 
  A distinction is made between:
    * **Active protection**: must be activated by the operators themselves (emergency stops, for example) and/or worn (PPE)
    * **Passive protection**: intervenes even without human command
* - **Guard**
  - A physical barrier, designed as part of the machine, to provide protection.
* - **Risk**
  - A combination of the probability and severity of an injury or health damage that may arise in a 
  hazardous situation.
* - **Residual risk**
  - Risk that remains after protective and preventive measures have been taken.
* - **Intended use**
  - Use of a machine in accordance with the information provided in the operating instructions.
* - **Reasonably foreseeable misuse**
  - Use of a machine in a manner not intended by the designer, but which may result from easily 
  foreseeable human behaviour.
```
