# **Glossary**

```{list-table}
:header-rows: 1
:widths: 25 75

* - Term
  - Definition
* - **Lifting accessories**
  - Components or equipment not attached to lifting machines, which allow the load to be gripped, placed between the machine and the load or on the load itself, or intended to become an integral part of the load and to be placed on the market separately. Slings and their components are also considered lifting accessories.
* - **Failure**
  - A fault of any kind that prevents the normal operation of a machine, a plant, etc.
* - **Chains, ropes or belts**
  - Elements designed and built for lifting purposes as an integral part of lifting machines or lifting accessories.
* - **Damage**
  - Any negative consequence arising from the occurrence of the hazardous event.
* - **D.P.I.**
  - The term Personal Protective Equipment (abbreviated PPE) means products whose function is to safeguard the worker (operator, maintenance staff, technician, etc.) who wears them or otherwise carries them, from risks to health and safety.
* - **Machine**
  - Assembly equipped, or intended to be equipped, with a drive system, consisting of parts or components, at least one of which is movable, joined together rigidly for a specific application.
* - **Malfunction**
  - Faulty or inadequate operation of a machine or of one of its elements in performing a given function.
* - **Hazard**
  - Potential source of harm which, if not avoided, entails a risk to the safety and health of exposed persons.
* - **Protection**
  - Defence against whatever could cause harm. An element placed between the person who may suffer harm and the cause of that harm, for hazards that cannot reasonably be eliminated or risks that cannot be sufficiently reduced during design. We distinguish:
    * **Active protection**: must be activated by the operators themselves (emergency stops, for example) and/or worn (PPE)
    * **Passive protection**: acts even without human command
* - **Guard**
  - Physical barrier, designed as part of the machine, to provide protection.
* - **Risk**
  - Combination of the probability and the severity of an injury or damage to health that may arise in a hazardous situation.
* - **Residual risk**
  - Risk that remains after protective and preventive measures have been taken.
* - **Intended use**
  - Use of a machine in accordance with the information provided in the instructions for use.
* - **Reasonably foreseeable misuse**
  - Use of a machine in a way not intended by the designer, but which may result from readily predictable human behaviour.
```
