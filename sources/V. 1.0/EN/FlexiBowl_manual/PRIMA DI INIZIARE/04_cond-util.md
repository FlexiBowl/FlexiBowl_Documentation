- be used in accordance with the instructions and limits of use given in this manual.

- it is used within the limits declared in the contract and in this manual;
- the procedures in the user manual are followed;
- routine maintenance is carried out at the times and in the ways indicated;
- extraordinary maintenance is carried out promptly when necessary;
- the safety devices are not removed and/or bypassed.

## Reasonably foreseeable misuse

The reasonably foreseeable misuse is listed below:
- processing liquid components and fine grit;
- changing working parameters in ways that compromise safety;
- transporting people;
- using the machine as a support point;
- using the machine so as to obtain production values above the prescribed limits;
- modifying / tampering with the electrical and pneumatic connections of the machine or any other of its components;
- using the machine with a product other than that listed in the "Intended (correct) use";
- using the machine in a way other than that provided for in the paragraph "Intended (correct) use".

Any use of the machine other than that intended must be authorized in advance in writing by the Manufacturer. In the absence of such written authorization, the use is to be considered "improper use"; the Manufacturer therefore declines all liability for any damage caused to property or persons and considers any type of warranty on the machine void.

::: {important}
Improper use of the FlexiBowl® excludes any liability of the Manufacturer.
:::

## Permitted environmental conditions

The environment in which the FlexiBowl® is installed and used is indoors, protected from atmospheric agents such as rain, hail, snow, fog, airborne dust, combustible dust, protected from aggressive agents such as corrosive vapors or sources of excessive heat, and must not be classified ATEX. Use of the FlexiBowl®, of the associated control systems and of the drive equipment in conditions other than those listed is not permitted. In particular, the installation and operating environment must not present:
- Exposure to corrosive fumes;
- Exposure to excessive humidity (above 85 %) and rapid changes in relative humidity (above 0.005 p.u./h);
- Exposure to excessive dust;
- Exposure to abrasive dust;
- Exposure to oily vapors;
- Exposure to explosive mixtures of dust or gas;
- Exposure to salt-laden air;
- Exposure to abnormal vibrations, impacts or shocks;
- Exposure to weather outside the permitted limits or to dripping water;
- Exposure to unusual transport or storage conditions;
- Exposure to high or rapid temperature changes (above 5K/h);
- Presence of nuclear radiation.

The FlexiBowl® is designed and built to operate safely in the following environmental conditions:

:header-rows: 1

  - Ambient temperature during operation

  - -10 ÷ 65 °C
  - 85%

:::

## Obligations and prohibitions

### Obligations of the users

The user (entrepreneur or employer) **must**:
- take into account the capabilities and conditions of the operators in relation to their health and safety;
- provide personal protective equipment suited to the individual procedures;
- provide compliant lifting equipment and procedures;
- require each worker to comply with the company's safety rules and provisions and with the use of the collective and personal protective equipment made available to them;
- train the personnel on the procedures to follow in the event of an accident;
- train the personnel on the residual risks present;
- train the personnel on the devices provided for operator safety;
- train the personnel on the risks of noise emission in the work environment;
- train the personnel on the general accident-prevention rules laid down by European directives and by the legislation of the country of destination of the machine.

::: {important}
Allow only personnel who have read this manual and been suitably trained to work on the machine.
::: 

### Obligations of the assigned personnel (operators/maintenance staff/technicians)

The personnel **must**:
- Always carry out maintenance work with the machines switched off. Do not lubricate moving parts.
- When the machine is running, do not work near it wearing chains, bracelets, ties or other clothing that could get caught in the mechanisms.
- Operators with long hair are required to tie it back so that it cannot get caught.
- Always carry out work on the electrical panel, junction boxes, cables and all components of the electrical system with the main switch off.
- When starting the machine, make sure that nobody is inside the hazardous zones.
- During operation, pay the utmost attention that nobody can directly access the moving parts.
- Use the protective devices provided by the employer appropriately.
- Immediately report any deficiencies of the safety devices to the employer, the manager or the supervisor.

### Prohibitions for the assigned personnel (operators/maintenance staff/technicians)

In particular, the personnel **must not**:
- use the machine improperly, i.e. for purposes other than those indicated in the "Intended use" paragraph;
- remove or modify the safety or signaling devices without authorization;
- carry out on their own initiative operations or maneuvers that are not within their competence or that may compromise their own safety or that of other workers;
- wear bracelets, rings or necklaces that may dangle and be dragged by moving parts, creating a hazard for the operator;
- replace or change the speeds of the machine components without being permitted to do so by a supervisor;
- modify the machine cycle;
- modify the electrical connections to bypass the internal safety functions;
- use the machine if it has not been installed in accordance with the regulations in force;
- use the machine as a support point even when it is not operating (at the risk of falling and/or damaging the machines themselves);
- use the machine outside the permitted environmental conditions (see paragraph {ref}`"Transport and handling" <trasporto>`).

:::{warning}
ARS S.r.l. is not liable for damage caused to property or persons if:
- it is established that the machine has been used in one of the non-permitted environments;
- the obligations and prohibitions described here have not been complied with.
:::
