# **Warranty**

```{important}
   The complete warranty terms are included in the **sales contract**.
   In the event of discrepancies, the conditions established in the contract take absolute precedence over what is stated in this section.
```
---

## General Conditions of Validity

For the warranty to be considered valid, the following operating requirements must be met:

```{list-table}
   :widths: 30 70
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Area
     - Mandatory Condition
   * - **Receipt**
     - Opening of packaging using suitable tools without damaging the system.
   * - **Commissioning**
     - Installation and start-up carried out by qualified and trained technicians.
   * - **Use**
     - Operation of the machine within the contractual and technical limits specified.
   * - **Maintenance**
     - Carried out within the scheduled timeframes using original **ARS S.r.l.** spare parts and authorised personnel.
```
---

## Exclusions and Warranty Voidance

The warranty is considered **null and void** upon the occurrence of one or more of the following conditions:

```{warning}
   **Tampering and Safety**

   * Removal or tampering with safety devices (guards, sensors, microswitches).
   * Unauthorised modifications to the machine control software.
   * Tampering with electrical or pneumatic circuits.
   * Removal of the identification plate or safety pictograms.
```
```{error}
   **Improper Use and Negligence**

   * Failure to comply with safety regulations and the instructions in this manual.
   * Use by untrained or unauthorised personnel.
   * Failure to implement the scheduled maintenance plan.
   * Installation in unsuitable premises or power supply faults (electrical/pneumatic).
   * Modifications or repairs carried out without the written authorisation of the Manufacturer.
```

```{note}
   Also excluded from the warranty are damages arising from exceptional events (floods, fires not caused by the machine) and disposal not in compliance with applicable regulations.
```
