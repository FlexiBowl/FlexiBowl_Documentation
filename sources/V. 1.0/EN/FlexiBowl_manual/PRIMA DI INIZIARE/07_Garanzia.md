# **Warranty**

```{important}
   The complete warranty terms are included in the **sales contract**. 
   In the event of discrepancies, the conditions set out in the contract take absolute precedence over what is stated in this section.
```
---

## General Conditions of Validity

For the warranty to be considered valid, the following operating requirements must be met:

```{list-table}
   :widths: 30 70
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Area
     - Mandatory Condition
   * - **Receipt**
     - Opening of the packaging with suitable tools without damaging the system.
   * - **Commissioning**
     - Installation and start-up carried out by qualified and trained technicians.
   * - **Use**
     - Use of the machine within the specified contractual and technical limits.
   * - **Maintenance**
     - Carried out within the scheduled times with original **ARS S.r.l.** spare parts and by authorized personnel.
```
---

## Exclusions and Forfeiture of the Warranty

The warranty is considered **null and void** if one or more of the following conditions occur:

```{warning} 
   **Tampering and Safety**
   
   * Removal or tampering with safety devices (guards, sensors, microswitches).
   * Unauthorized modifications to the machine control software.
   * Tampering with the electrical or pneumatic circuits.
   * Removal of the identification plate or safety pictograms.
```
```{error}
   **Improper Use and Negligence**
   
   * Failure to comply with the safety regulations and the instructions in the manual.
   * Use by untrained or unauthorized personnel.
   * Failure to implement the scheduled maintenance plan.
   * Installation in unsuitable premises or power supply faults (electrical/pneumatic).
   * Modifications or repairs without written authorization from the Manufacturer.
```

```{note}
   Damage resulting from exceptional events (floods, fires not caused by the machine) and disposal that does not comply with regulations is also excluded from the warranty.
```
