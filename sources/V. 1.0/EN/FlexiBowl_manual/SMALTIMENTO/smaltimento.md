# **Disposal and end of life**

:::{warning}
Decommissioning and dismantling operations must be entrusted to personnel specialised in such activities. In particular, only the person in charge of the dismantling phase and of end-of-life disposal may carry out:
- mechanical and electrical disconnection of the parts according to the disassembly instructions and design diagrams.
- transport of the parts from the installation site to the disposal centre for separation of the parts.
:::

The materials the machine is made of are essentially:
- painted, plastic-coated or galvanised ferritic steel;
- 300/400 series stainless steel;
- polyethylene plastic;
- elastomers, PTFE, graphite;
- gear oil;
- lubrication grease;
- electric motors;
- electric cables with their sheaths;
- electronic control and actuation devices.
- etc.

:::{important}
The machine does not contain hazardous components or substances that require special removal procedures.
:::

## Decommissioning

If the FlexiBowl® is not to be used for a long period, it must be made safe and stored. Proceed as described:

:::{list-table}
:widths: 15 60
:header-rows: 1

* - Step
  - Operation

* - 1
  - Disconnect the electrical power supply.

* - 2
  - Disconnect the pneumatic power supply.

* - 3
  - Protect the electrical equipment, which is particularly subject to the effects of time and dust.

:::

:::{important}
For the decommissioning of machines from external suppliers, refer to the sub-supplier manuals for those machines, attached to this manual.
:::

## **Responsible disposal**

:::{warning}
Disconnect the electrical and pneumatic power supply of the machine.
:::

:::{warning}
For the disassembly of off-the-shelf parts or sub-supplied material that are part of the machine supplied by ARS S.r.l., please refer to the relevant supplier manual.
:::

The demolition of the machine must be entrusted to qualified personnel, each within their own area of competence. When the machine is to be disposed of, it must be made safe. Under the "WEEE" Directive 2012/19/EU, if the purchased component/equipment is marked with the following symbol of a crossed-out wheeled waste bin, this means that at the end of its useful life the product must be collected separately from other waste.

:::{warning}
When the product reaches the end of its life cycle, it must be disposed of in accordance with the regulations in force on waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE). Please observe the laws in force on disposal in the country where the machine is installed.
:::

### **Components subject to special disposal**

:::{note}
**Components subject to special disposal:**
- Electronic boards (VisionController): WEEE category 6
- Industrial camera: WEEE category 6
- LED illuminators: WEEE category 5
- Cables and connectors: dispose of with electrical materials
:::
