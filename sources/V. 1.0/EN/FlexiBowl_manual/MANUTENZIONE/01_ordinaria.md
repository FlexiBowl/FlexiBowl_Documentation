# **Routine Maintenance**

When delivered to the user, the FlexiBowl® is already adjusted to work correctly; however, to ensure good operation over time, periodic and preventive checks and maintenance must be carried out. Routine maintenance includes inspections, checks and interventions that, to prevent failures, keep under control:
- the mechanical condition of the machine,
- the cleanliness of the machine.

The following tables list a series of checks and interventions to be carried out according to a recommended schedule. The intervals of the routine maintenance operations indicated refer to normal operating conditions, i.e. conditions complying with the intended conditions of use. The following table lists a series of routine maintenance procedures valid for all types of machines produced by ARS S.r.l. The operator must consider only the procedures relating to the machine covered by this manual.

:::{important}
For the routine maintenance of machines from external suppliers, refer to the sub-supplier manuals of those machines, attached to this manual.
:::

:::{important}
When fastening screws, always use LOCTITE 243 to ensure a perfect fixing (except for the screws of the surface or rigid disc flange).
:::
