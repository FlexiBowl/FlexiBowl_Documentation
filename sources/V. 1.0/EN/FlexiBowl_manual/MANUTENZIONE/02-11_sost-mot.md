:::{warning}
Disconnect the electrical power supply before proceeding with the replacement of the motor unit.
:::

:::{warning}
Some parts involved in the maintenance process described here may have reached {ref}`high temperatures <temp>` during operation; make sure to leave the FlexiBowl® switched off long enough for all its parts to be handled safely.
:::

:::{important}
When fitting the motor unit to the FlexiBowl®, it is important to use the same fixing screws and to apply Loctite 243.
:::

## FlexiBowl® 200

On the FlexiBowl® 200 the motor is direct drive, and the spare part supplied consists of the motor only.

### What is needed?

:::{list-table}
:widths: 40 40
:header-rows: 1

* - Item/equipment
  - Quantity

* - H5 hex key
  - 1

* - H4 hex key
  - 1

* - Replacement motor unit
  - 1

:::

### Procedure

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the flip shield assembly |
| 2 | {ref}`Remove the surface or rigid disc <sost-disco>` |
| 3 | {ref}`Remove the Backlight <sostbl>` |
| 4 | Remove the front support screw of the Backlight box |
| 5 | Remove the four screws fixing the table and the two screws fixing the shield distributor to it |
| 6 | Lift the table and disconnect all the motor connections |
| 7 | Remove the screws fixing the motor flange to the table |
| 8 | Rotate the disc-holder flange until the locking set screws are visible, and remove them |
| 9 | Unscrew the screws fixing the motor to the flange and dismantle the motor |
| 10 | Remove the spacer and replace the motor |
| 11 | When the replacement is finished, refit all the components |

## FlexiBowl® 350

For the FlexiBowl® 350 there is a motor unit that also includes the mounting flange and the entire kinematic chain that transmits the motion to the rigid disc support, and the spare part supplied consists of the unit as a whole.

### What is needed?

:::{list-table}
:widths: 40 40
:header-rows: 1

* - Item/equipment
  - Quantity

* - H5 hex key
  - 1

* - H4 hex key
  - 1

* - Replacement motor unit
  - 1

:::

### Procedure

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the flip shield assembly |
| 2 | {ref}`Remove the surface or rigid disc <sost-disco>` |
| 3 | {ref}`Remove the Backlight <sostbl>` |
| 4 | Remove the front support screw of the Backlight box |
| 5 | Remove the four screws fixing the table and the two screws fixing the shield distributor to it |
| 6 | Lift the table and disconnect all the motor connections |
| 7 | Remove the screws fixing the motor flange to the table |
| 8 | Remove and replace the motor unit |
| 9 | When the replacement is finished, refit all the components |

## FlexiBowl® 500-650-800-1200

The larger-size FlexiBowl® units are fitted with a motor unit which, like that of the FlexiBowl® 350, comprises the entire kinematic assembly. In this case too, the spare part is the entire unit.

### What is needed?

:::{list-table}
:widths: 40 40
:header-rows: 1

* - Item/equipment
  - Quantity

* - H2.5 hex key
  - 1

* - Replacement Backlight
  - 1

:::

### Standard motor unit replacement

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the flip shield assembly |
| 2 | {ref}`Remove the surface or rigid disc <sost-disco>` |
| 3 | Remove the cover plate of the flip unit (use two of the fixing screws to remove it through the dedicated threaded holes) |
| 4 | Remove the pneumatic connections and dismantle the flip unit |
| 5 | Replace the damaged flip unit with the new one |
| 6 | When the replacement is finished, refit all the components |
