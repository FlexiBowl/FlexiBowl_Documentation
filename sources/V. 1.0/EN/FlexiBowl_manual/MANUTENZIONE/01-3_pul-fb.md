# **General Cleaning of the FlexiBowl®**

To ensure the FlexiBowl® always operates correctly, it is important to keep it in a good state of cleanliness. To carry out the general cleaning of the machine, proceed as described below:

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Disconnect the electrical and pneumatic power supply from the FlexiBowl®. |
| 2 | Manually remove any product residues |
| 3 | Remove dirt using non-flammable, non-toxic commercial solvents |
| 4 | If necessary, use a vacuum cleaner to remove residues present on the surface or rigid disc |
| 5 | Once cleaning is finished, restore the machine connections. |

:::{important}
The general cleaning of the FlexiBowl® must be carried out every time the type of component to be processed is changed, so as to remove any residues left over from the previous processing.
:::
