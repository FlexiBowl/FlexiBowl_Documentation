# **Cleaning the Disc**

:::{warning}
Disconnect the electrical power supply before proceeding with cleaning.
:::

To clean the surface or the rigid disc, proceed as follows:

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | {ref}`Remove the surface or rigid disc <sost-disco>` |
| 2 | Clean the surface or rigid disc using alcohol or degreaser |
| 3 | Clean the area on which the surface or rigid disc rests using degreaser |
| 4 | When cleaning is finished, {ref}`refit the surface or rigid disc <sost-disco>` |
