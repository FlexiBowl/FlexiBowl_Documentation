# [MEC] **Indirect blows (FlexiBowl® 500E only)**

Since radial blows are not compatible with the FlexiBowl® 500E, indirect blows can be used to still make it possible to use an air jet as a system for deflecting parts away from the edge of the rigid disc. These consist of two nozzles that are assembled on the wall of a dedicated flip screen, and whose air jet is directed towards the inner face of the disc edge, so as to create a turbulence zone that pushes the components towards the center of the FlexiBowl® work area.

:::{figure} ../../../../_shared/media/images/sofind500.PNG
:width: 100%
:align: center
:::

## Recommended applications

They have essentially the same function as radial blows, and are therefore recommended for the same situations.

## Mounting procedure

:::{warning}
Disconnect the electrical power supply before proceeding with the mounting of the blows.
:::

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the entire screen-flip barrier assembly by unscrewing the four fixing screws |
| 2 | Replace the screen with the indirect blow assembly |
| 3 | Fix the new indirect blow-flip barrier assembly in place |

## Adjustment procedure

The blow intensity is adjusted via software by changing the "blow pressure" value. The intensity of each blow can then be differentiated independently by means of the adjustment needles located under the flip screen support.
