# **Quick Emptying**

Quick Emptying is a system that allows the FlexiBowl® to lower a portion of the containment ring in order to evacuate the residual components from the FlexiBowl® before a production changeover. The option can be activated via digital I/O or via software, and the position of the movable portion of the containment ring is known thanks to sensors that determine whether it is open or closed. The Quick Emptying sequence is preconfigured and consists of fast clockwise and counterclockwise movements.

:::{note}

:::

## Activation procedure 

The Quick Emptying option is made available automatically if purchased. No activation procedure is required; you can simply follow the instructions on the [Quick Emptying](emptying) page.
