# **Standard Diverters**

Standard diverters are polyethylene blocks with shaped surfaces designed to create a sliding effect for the components as they move together with the surface or rigid disc. The idea behind diverters (standard or of other types) is to change the position of the parts along the radius of the FlexiBowl®, so as to maximize the effectiveness of the flip and to keep them at a distance from the containment ring and from the disc clamping cap that allows the robot to pick them, without the *end effector* colliding with either of the two.

## Recommended applications 
Option fitted by default; it is suitable for handling components without protrusions small enough to get caught between the diverter and the disc.

## Mounting procedure

:::{warning} Attenzione
Make sure the FlexiBowl® is **switched off and stationary** before proceeding with the mounting.
:::

## Adjustment procedure

:::{warning} Attenzione
Make sure the FlexiBowl® is **switched off and stationary** before proceeding with the adjustment.
:::

### Diverters for rigid disc (FlexiBowl® 350-500E)

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Loosen the screws for radial and vertical adjustment |
| 2 | Place a sheet of paper between the disc and the diverter to keep it at the right distance, and tighten the vertical adjustment screws |
| 3 | Place a sheet of paper between the outer surface of the diverter and the inner surface of the disc edge to keep it at the right distance, and tighten the %radial adjustment screws |

## Checking correct operation 

The diverters are correctly adjusted when the surface or rigid disc slides without meeting resistance and the components being handled do not get caught between the two.

For optimal operation, it is also necessary to check that:

- the **pre-flip** diverter brings the parts into the zone of maximum flip efficiency, i.e. the central one;
- the **post-flip** diverter moves the parts away from the edge just enough for the robot to pick them, even if a gripper that might hit the edge itself is used.
