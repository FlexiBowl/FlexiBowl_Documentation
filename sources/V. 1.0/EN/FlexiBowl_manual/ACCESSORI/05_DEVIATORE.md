# [MEC] **Standard Diverters**

Standard diverters are polyethylene blocks with shaped surfaces designed to create a slide effect for the components as they move together with the surface or rigid disc. The idea behind diverters (standard or of other types) is to change the position of the parts along the radius of the FlexiBowl®, so as to maximize the effectiveness of the flip and keep them at a distance from the containment ring and from the disc-clamping cap that allows the robot to pick them, without the *end effector* colliding with either of the two.

## Recommended applications 
Mounted by default, this option is suitable for handling components without protrusions small enough to get stuck between the diverter and the disc.

## Mounting procedure

:::{warning} Warning
Make sure that the FlexiBowl® is **switched off and stationary** before proceeding with the mounting.
:::

%### Diverters for rigid disc (FlexiBowl® 350-500E)

%:::{note}
%On FlexiBowl® units with a rigid disc, the diverters are not fixed by default.
%:::

%The diverters are fixed to the dedicated holes at the sides of the flip screen by means of two M6x22 screws each.

%:::{figure} ../../../../_shared/media/images/deviatoridisco_fissaggio.PNG
%:width: 100%
%:::

%### Diverters for surface (FlexiBowl® 500-650-800-1200)

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Place the diverter in the dedicated slot in the containment ring |
| 2 | Using a flat screwdriver, tighten the adjustment screws until the diverter is at the desired height |
| 3 | Use the locking screws to lock the diverter in position |

:width: 100%

## Adjustment procedure

:::{warning} Warning
Make sure that the FlexiBowl® is **switched off and stationary** before proceeding with the adjustment.
:::

%### Diverters for rigid disc (FlexiBowl® 350-500E)

%| Step | Operation |
%|:----:|-----------|
%| 1 | Loosen the screws for radial and vertical adjustment |
%| 2 | Place a sheet of paper between the disc and the diverter to keep it at the right distance and tighten the screws for vertical adjustment |
%| 3 | Place a sheet of paper between the outer surface of the diverter and the inner surface of the disc edge to keep it at the right distance and tighten the screws for %radial adjustment |

%:::{figure} ../../../../_shared/media/images/deviatoridisco_regolazione.PNG
%:width: 100%
%:::

%### Diverters for surface (FlexiBowl® 500-650-800-1200)

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the plugs on the diverter cartridge |
| 2 | Loosen the fixing screws (2) |
| 3 | Loosen or tighten the adjustment screws (1) until the lower surface of the diverter is ~0.1mm from the surface of the disc |
| 4 | Tighten the fixing screws (2) to lock the diverter in position |

## Verifying correct operation 

The diverters are correctly adjusted when the surface or rigid disc slides without meeting resistance and the components to be handled do not get stuck between the two.
