# **External Air Filtration Kit**

For its pneumatic supply, the FlexiBowl® requires clean compressed air in accordance with **ISO 8573-1:2010 class 1.3.2**, that is:

* Solid particles must be ≤0.1μm in size;

* Pressure dew point ≤-20°C (no condensation or frost);

* Oil concentration (including aerosols and vapors) ≤0.1 mg/m³.

If it is not possible to guarantee this level of air cleanliness, the external filtration kit can be purchased and connected directly upstream of the FlexiBowl®.

:::{note}
The kit has an Ø8 fitting on both inlet and outlet and includes a reducer for the outlet from Ø8 to Ø6 for the FlexiBowl® 200 and 350. A spare filter is also included for each of the two filter units.
