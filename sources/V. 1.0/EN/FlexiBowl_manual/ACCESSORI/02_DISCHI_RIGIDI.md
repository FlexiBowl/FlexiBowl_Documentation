# [MEC] **Rigid discs**

Rigid discs are the alternative to surfaces suited to the FlexiBowl® in edge version:
- **FlexiBowl® 200**
- **FlexiBowl® 350**
- **FlexiBowl® 500E**

Their rigidity changes the way the flip works: instead of acting on the components in a practically direct manner through the movement of the surface, it causes a vibration on the disc that is ideal for sorting smaller components. They also feature an integrated edge, which makes them even more suitable for processing small components since it eliminates the gap that can form between a surface and the normal containment ring.
