# [MEC] **Wedge Diverter**

The Wedge Diverter consists of a bump positioned upstream of the flip area, under the surface, to create a rise that brings the components back to a zone where the flip can be more effective.

:::{figure} ../../../../_shared/media/images/wedge.PNG
:width: 100%
:align: center
:::

## Recommended applications 

A solution to adopt for handling delicate components that can break or deform under the action of the flip. It is particularly suitable for parts that roll or slide easily. 

## Mounting procedure

:::{warning} Warning
Make sure that the FlexiBowl® is **switched off and stationary** before proceeding with the mounting.
:::

:::{figure} ../../../../_shared/media/images/wedgemount.PNG
:width: 100%
:align: center
:::

### FB500: 

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the fixing screws of both diverters |
| 2 | Unscrew the adjustment screws to remove both diverters |
| 3 | Fit the plug cartridge in place of the post-flip diverter |
| 4 | Fit the wedge diverter assembly in place of the pre-flip diverter |

### FB650-800: 

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the fixing screws of both diverters |
| 2 | Unscrew the adjustment screws to remove both diverters |
| 3 | Making sure the square nuts are in place, fit the wedge diverter cartridge in place of the pre-flip diverter |
| 4 | Replace the ring sector upstream of the pre-flip diverter with the one supplied in the kit |
| 5 | Fix the wall with the screws supplied |

### FB1200: 

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the fixing screws of both diverters |
| 2 | Unscrew the adjustment screws to remove both diverters |
| 3 | Remove the ring sector on which the pre-flip diverter was mounted and replace it with the ring sector included in the kit |
| 4 | Fit the plug cartridge in place of the post-flip diverter |
| 5 | Making sure the square nuts are in place, fit the wedge diverter cartridge in place of the pre-flip diverter |
| 6 | Fix the wall with the screws supplied |

## Adjustment procedure 

The wedge diverter does not require adjustment 

## Verifying correct operation 

Before starting regular use of the accessory, check that the disc slides correctly over the wedge diverter.
