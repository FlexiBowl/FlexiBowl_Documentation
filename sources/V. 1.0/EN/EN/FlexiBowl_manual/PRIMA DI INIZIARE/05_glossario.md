# **Glossary** 

```{list-table}
:header-rows: 1
:widths: 25 75

* - Term
  - Definition
* - **Lifting accessories**
  - Components or equipment not attached to lifting machines, which allow the load to be gripped, placed between the machine and the load or on the load itself, or intended to become an integral part of the load and placed on the market separately. 
  Slings and their components are also considered lifting accessories. 
* - **Breakdown**
  - A fault of any kind that prevents the normal operation of a machine, a 
  plant, etc. 
* - **Chains, ropes or belts**
  - Elements designed and built for lifting purposes as an integral part of lifting machines or lifting accessories. 
* - **Harm**
  - Any negative consequence resulting from the occurrence of the hazardous event. 
* - **PPE**
  - The term Personal Protective Equipment (abbreviated PPE) refers to products whose function is to safeguard the worker (operator, maintenance technician, engineer, etc.) who wears them or otherwise carries them, from risks to health and safety. 
* - **Machine**
  - An assembly, fitted with or intended to be fitted with a drive system, consisting of linked parts or components, at least one of which moves, joined together firmly for a specific application. 
* - **Malfunction**
  - Defective or inadequate operation of a machine or of one of its elements in performing a given function. 
* - **Hazard**
  - Potential source of harm that, if not avoided, poses a risk to the safety and health of exposed persons. 
* - **Protection**
  - Defense against what could cause harm. An element placed between the person who may suffer harm and what may cause it, for hazards that cannot reasonably be eliminated or for risks that cannot be sufficiently reduced during design. 
  We distinguish:
    * **Active protection**: must be activated by the operators themselves (emergency stops, for example) and/or worn (PPE)
    * **Passive protection**: operates even without human command  
* - **Guard**
  - Physical barrier, designed as part of the machine, to provide protection. 
* - **Risk**
  - Combination of the probability and the severity of an injury or damage to health that may arise in a hazardous situation.
* - **Residual risk**
  - Risk that remains after protective and preventive measures have been taken. 
* - **Intended use**
  - Use of a machine in accordance with the information provided in the instructions for use. 
* - **Reasonably foreseeable misuse**
  - Use of a machine in a way not intended by the designer, but which may result from readily predictable human behavior. 
```
