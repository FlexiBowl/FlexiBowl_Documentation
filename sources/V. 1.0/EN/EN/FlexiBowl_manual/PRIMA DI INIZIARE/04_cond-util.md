## Reasonably foreseeable misuse

The reasonably foreseeable misuse is listed below: 
- processing liquid components and fine grit; 
- changing working parameters in a way that undermines safety; 
- transporting people; 
- using the machine as a support point; 
- using the machine so as to obtain production values higher than the prescribed limits; 
- modifying / tampering with the electrical and pneumatic connections of the machine or any other of its components; 
- using the machine with a product other than that listed in the “Intended use (correct)”;  
- using the machine differently from what is provided for in the paragraph “Intended use (correct)”. 

Any use of the machine other than the intended one must be authorized in advance in writing by the 
Manufacturer. In the absence of such written authorization, the use is to be considered “improper use”; therefore the Manufacturer 
accepts no liability for any damage caused to property or persons and considers any type of 
warranty on the machine to be void. 

::: {important}
Improper use of the FlexiBowl® excludes any liability of the Manufacturer.
:::

## Permitted environmental conditions

The environment in which the FlexiBowl® is installed and used is indoors, protected from atmospheric agents such as: rain, hail, snow, 
fog, suspended dust, combustible dust, protected from aggressive agents such as corrosive vapors or sources of 
excessive heat, and must not be classified as ATEX. 
Use of the FlexiBowl®, of the associated control systems and of the drive equipment under conditions different from those 
listed is not permitted. 
In particular, the installation and use environment must not present: 
- Exposure to corrosive fumes; 
- Exposure to excessive humidity (above 85%) and rapid changes in relative humidity (above 0.005 p.u./h);  
- Exposure to excessive dust; 
- Exposure to abrasive dust; 
- Exposure to oily vapors; 
- Exposure to explosive mixtures of dust or gas; 
- Exposure to salt-laden air; 
- Exposure to abnormal vibrations, impacts or shocks; 
- Exposure to bad weather beyond the permitted limits, or dripping; 
- Exposure to unusual transport or storage conditions; 
- Exposure to high or rapid temperature variations (above 5K/h); 
- Presence of nuclear radiation.

The FlexiBowl® is designed and built to operate safely under the following environmental conditions:

:header-rows: 1

* - FlexiBowl® protection rating
  - Ambient temperature during operation

:::

## Obligations and prohibitions

### Obligations of users

The user (entrepreneur or employer) **must**: 
- take into account the capabilities and conditions of the operators in relation to their health and safety; 
- provide the personal protective equipment suited to the individual procedures; 
- provide lifting equipment and procedures that comply with regulations; 
- require individual workers to comply with company rules and provisions on safety and on the use of the collective and personal protective equipment made available to them; 
- instruct personnel on the procedures to follow in case of accident; 
- instruct personnel on the residual risks present; 
- instruct personnel on the devices provided for operator safety;  
- instruct personnel on the risks of noise emission in the work environment;  
- instruct personnel on the general accident-prevention rules laid down by European directives and by the legislation of the country of destination of the machine. 

::: {important}
Allow only personnel who have read this manual and have been properly 
trained to work on the machine.
::: 

### Obligations of assigned personnel (operators/maintenance workers/technicians)

Personnel **must**: 
- Always carry out maintenance work with the machines switched off. Do not lubricate moving parts. 
- When the machine is running, do not work near it wearing chains, bracelets, ties, or other clothing that could get caught in the mechanisms. 
- Operators with long hair must tie it back so that it cannot get caught. 
- Always carry out work on the electrical panel, junction boxes, cables and all components of the electrical system with the main switch turned off. 
- When starting the machine, make sure that no one is inside the hazardous areas. 
- During operation, take the greatest care that no one can directly access the moving parts. 
- Use appropriately the protective devices made available by the employer. 
- Immediately report any deficiencies of the safety devices to the employer, manager or supervisor. 

### Prohibitions for assigned personnel (operators/maintenance workers/technicians)

In particular, personnel **must not**: 
- use the machine improperly, i.e. for uses other than those indicated in the paragraph “Intended use”; 
- remove or modify the safety or signaling devices without authorization; 
- carry out on their own initiative operations or maneuvers that are not within their competence or that may compromise their own safety or that of other workers; 
- wear bracelets, rings or chains that may dangle and be dragged by moving parts, creating danger for the operator; 
- replace or change the speeds of the machine components without being permitted to do so by a supervisor;  
- modify the machine cycle;  
- modify the electrical connections to bypass the internal safety devices; 
- use the machine if it has not been installed in accordance with the regulations in force; 
- use the machine as a support point, even when not running (risk of falling and/or of damaging the machines themselves); 
- use the machine outside the permitted environmental conditions (see paragraph {ref}`"Transport and handling" <trasporto>`). 

:::{warning}
ARS S.r.l. is not liable for damage caused to property or persons if: 
- it is established that the machine has been used in one of the non-permitted environments; 
- the obligations and prohibitions described here have not been observed. 
:::
