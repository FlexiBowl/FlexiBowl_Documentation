:::{note}
*The minimum supply pressure for the FlexiBowl® 1200 becomes 3 bar if it is fitted with emptying.
:::

:::{note}
**Average consumption measured with the flip only. Installing blows may increase consumption by up to 75%.
:::

:::{warning}
Using non-compliant air can cause malfunctions, reduced performance and premature wear of the pneumatic components.
:::

An **external air filtration kit** is optionally available on request. 
The kit is recommended when the quality of the compressed air of the customer's plant is unknown or not guaranteed, and it allows the required quality class to be reached.
