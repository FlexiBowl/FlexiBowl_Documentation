# **Disposal and End of Life**

:::{warning}
Decommissioning and dismantling operations must be entrusted to personnel specialized in such activities. In 
particular, only the person in charge of the dismantling and end-of-life disposal phase may carry out: 
- mechanical and electrical disconnection of the parts according to the disassembly instructions and design 
diagrams. 
- transport of the parts from the installation site to the disposal center for the separation of the 
parts.
:::

The materials the machine is made of are essentially:  
- painted, plastic-coated or galvanized ferritic steel;  
- 300/400 series stainless steel;  
- polyethylene plastic material; 
- elastomers, PTFE, graphite;  
- gear oil; 
- lubricating grease;  
- electric motors;  
- electric cables with their sheaths;  
- electronic control and actuation devices.  
- etc. 

:::{important}
The machine does not contain hazardous components or substances that require special 
removal procedures.
:::

## Decommissioning

If the FlexiBowl® is not to be used for a long period, it must be made safe and stored. 
Proceed as described:

:::{list-table}
:widths: 15 60
:header-rows: 1

* - Step
  - Operation

* - 1
  - Disconnect the electrical power supply.

* - 2
  - Disconnect the pneumatic power supply.

* - 3
  - Protect the electrical equipment particularly subject to the wear of time and dust.

:::

:::{important}
For the decommissioning of machines coming from external suppliers, refer to the 
sub-supplier manuals of the machines themselves, attached to this manual.
:::

## **Responsible disposal**

:::{warning}
Disconnect the electrical power supply and the pneumatic power supply of the machine. 
:::

:::{warning}
For the disassembly of commercial parts or sub-supplied material that are part of the machine supplied by ARS S.r.l., please refer to the relevant supplier's manual. 
:::

The demolition of the machine must be entrusted to qualified personnel, each for their own area of competence.  When the machine is to be disposed of, it must be made safe.
Pursuant to the “WEEE” Directive 2012/19/EU, if the component/equipment purchased is marked with the following symbol 
of the crossed-out wheeled waste bin, it means that the product at the end of its useful life must be collected 
separately from other waste. 

:::{warning}
When the product reaches the end of its life cycle, it must be disposed of in accordance with the regulations in force relating to waste electrical and electronic equipment (RAEE/WEEE). Compliance with the laws in force on disposal in the country where the machine is installed is required. 
:::

### **Components subject to special disposal**

:::{note}
**Components subject to special disposal:**
- Electronic boards (VisionController): WEEE category 6
- Industrial camera: WEEE category 6
- LED illuminators: WEEE category 5
- Cables and connectors: disposal with electrical materials
:::
