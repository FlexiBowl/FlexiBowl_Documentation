# **Flexitracking**

## Tracking operation

The Flexitracking option makes it possible to maximize the productivity of the FlexiBowl® by letting it run continuously and without interruptions. Instead of stopping at every cycle to take the picture and give the robot time to pick the parts present in the vision area, an encoder keeps track of the rotation angle covered by the surface or rigid disc between the moment the picture is taken and the moment the part is picked by the robot.

::::{tip}
Tracking operation shifts the picking area which, if it normally coincides with the vision area, will in this case be moved downstream. Refer to the {ref}`Layout Best Practice <layoutbp>` chapter for more information on the recommended placement of the robot and the other accessories in the case of standard operation and Flexitracking.
