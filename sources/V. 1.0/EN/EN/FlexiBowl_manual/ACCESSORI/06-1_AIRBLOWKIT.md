# [MEC] **Air Blow Kit**

:::{note}
Implementing the blows requires a modification of the FlexiBowl® pneumatic system; for more information refer to the {ref}`pneumatic diagrams <sc-pneum>`.
:::

The blow kit (or *Air Blow Kit*) is made up of the following parts:

## Radial blow

The radial blow consists of a cartridge that can be mounted in place of the standard diverter and has two radial holes at different heights: from one of the two, chosen by the Operator according to the size of the parts to be handled, an air jet comes out that moves the components away from the containment ring.

:::{figure} ../../../../_shared/media/images/sofrad500.PNG
:width: 100%
:align: center
:::

### Mounting procedure

:::{warning}
Disconnect the electrical power supply before proceeding with the mounting of the blows.
:::

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the diverter fixing screws |
| 2 | Unscrew the adjustment screws to remove the diverter |
| 3 | Place the solid rubber seal and the perforated one in their seats according to the desired blow height |
| 4 | Place the radial blow cartridge and fix it with the dedicated screws |

### Adjustment procedure

The radial blow can be adjusted both via software and mechanically, by acting on the dedicated grub screw located inside the cartridge with a 1.5mm Allen key, taking care to lock its position by tightening the pusher with a flat screwdriver.

## Central blow

The central blow consists of a nozzle mounted on the tip of the flip screen, whose air jet is directed so as to move the components away from the disc-clamping cap. Its purpose is twofold:

- Bring the components over the flip to maximize its efficiency;
- Prevent the components from being considered non-pickable because of excessive proximity to the disc-clamping cap, which would cause a robot collision.

:::{figure} ../../../../_shared/media/images/sofcen500.PNG
:width: 100%
:align: center
:::

### Mounting procedure

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the entire screen-flip barrier assembly by unscrewing the four fixing screws |
| 2 | Replace the screen with the central blow assembly |
| 3 | Fix the new central blow-flip barrier assembly in place |

### Adjustment procedure 

The blow intensity is adjusted via software.

## Recommended applications

They are to be preferred over the standard diverter when:
- there is a need to handle components with small features or protrusions that could get stuck in the gap between the diverter and the surface or rigid disc;
- an anti-roll surface or rigid disc is used.
