# [MEC] **Blows integrated in the flip screen (FlexiBowl® 200-350)**

The flip screens of the FlexiBowl® 200 and 350 are made by 3D printing. This allows the creation of internal channels ending in nozzles specially designed to direct the air jet so as to obtain the same behavior as the equivalent accessories, without the need for additional attachments to be mounted on the FlexiBowl®.

## Recommended applications

They have essentially the same function as the indirect blows and the central blow, and are therefore recommended for the same situations. On the FlexiBowl® 200 they are the only deflection system available.

## Mounting procedure

The blows integrated in the flip screen are mounted by default on the FlexiBowl® models they are compatible with.

## Adjustment procedure

The blow intensity is adjusted via software by changing the "blow pressure" value. The intensity of each blow can then be differentiated independently by means of the adjustment needles located under the flip screen support.
