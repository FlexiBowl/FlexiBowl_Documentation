# [MEC] **General Cleaning of the FlexiBowl®**

To always ensure the correct operation of the FlexiBowl®, it is important that it is kept clean. To carry out the general cleaning of the machine, proceed as described below: 

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Disconnect the electrical and pneumatic power supply from the FlexiBowl®. |
| 2 | Manually remove any product residues |
| 3 | Remove dirt using commercial non-flammable and non-toxic solvents |
| 4 | If necessary, use a vacuum cleaner to remove residues present on the surface or rigid disc |
| 5 | Once cleaning is finished, restore the machine connections. |

:::{important}
The general cleaning of the FlexiBowl® must be carried out every time the type of component to be processed is changed, so as to remove any residues from the previous processing.
:::
