# [MEC] **Routine Maintenance**

When it is delivered to the user, the FlexiBowl® is already adjusted to work correctly; however, to ensure its good 
operation over time, periodic and preventive checks and maintenance must be carried out. 
Routine maintenance includes inspections, checks and interventions which, in order to prevent faults, keep under control: 
- the mechanical condition of the machine, 
- the cleanliness of the machine. 

The following tables list a series of checks and interventions to be carried out following a recommended schedule. The intervals 
of the routine maintenance operations indicated refer to normal operating conditions, i.e. conditions corresponding to the 
intended conditions of use. 
The following table lists a series of routine maintenance procedures valid for all types of machines produced by 
ARS S.r.l. 
The operator must only take into account the procedures relating to the machine covered by this manual.

:::{important}
For the routine maintenance of machines coming from external suppliers, refer to the sub-supplier manuals of the machines themselves, attached to this manual.
:::

:::{important}
When fastening screws, always use LOCTITE 243 in order to achieve a perfect fixing (with the exception of the screws of the surface or rigid disc flange). 
:::
