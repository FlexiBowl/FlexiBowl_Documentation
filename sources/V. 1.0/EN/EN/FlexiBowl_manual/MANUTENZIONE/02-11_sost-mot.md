:::{warning}
Disconnect the electrical power supply before proceeding with the replacement of the motor assembly.
:::

:::{warning}
Some parts involved in the maintenance process described here may have reached {ref}`high temperatures <temp>` during operation; make sure to leave the FlexiBowl® switched off long enough for all its parts to be handled safely.
:::

:::{important}
When mounting the motor assembly on the FlexiBowl®, it is important to use the same fixing screws and apply Loctite 243.
:::

## FlexiBowl® 200

In the FlexiBowl® 200 the motor is direct-drive, and the spare part supplied consists of the motor only.

### What you need

:::{list-table}
:widths: 40 40
:header-rows: 1

* - Item/equipment
  - Quantity

* - H5 hex key
  - 1

* - H4 hex key
  - 1

* - Replacement motor assembly
  - 1

:::

### Procedure

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the flip screen assembly |
| 2 | {ref}`Remove the surface or rigid disc <sost-disco>` |
| 3 | {ref}`Remove the Backlight <sostbl>` |
| 4 | Remove the front support screw of the Backlight box |
| 5 | Remove the four fixing screws of the table and the two screws that fix the screen distributor to it |
| 6 | Lift the table and disconnect all the motor connections |
| 7 | Remove the screws that fix the motor flange to the table |
| 8 | Rotate the disc-holder flange until the locking grub screws are visible and remove them |
| 9 | Unscrew the screws that fix the motor to the flange and remove the motor |
| 10 | Remove the spacer and replace the motor |
| 11 | Once the replacement is finished, refit all the components |

## FlexiBowl® 350

For the FlexiBowl® 350 there is a motor assembly that also includes the fixing flange and the entire kinematic chain that transmits motion to the rigid disc support, and the spare part supplied consists of the assembly as a whole.

### What you need

:::{list-table}
:widths: 40 40
:header-rows: 1

* - Item/equipment
  - Quantity

* - H5 hex key
  - 1

* - H4 hex key
  - 1

* - Replacement motor assembly
  - 1

:::

### Procedure

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the flip screen assembly |
| 2 | {ref}`Remove the surface or rigid disc <sost-disco>` |
| 3 | {ref}`Remove the Backlight <sostbl>` |
| 4 | Remove the front support screw of the Backlight box |
| 5 | Remove the four fixing screws of the table and the two screws that fix the screen distributor to it |
| 6 | Lift the table and disconnect all the motor connections |
| 7 | Remove the screws that fix the motor flange to the table |
| 8 | Remove and replace the motor assembly |
| 9 | Once the replacement is finished, refit all the components |

## FlexiBowl® 500-650-800-1200

The larger FlexiBowl® sizes are equipped with a motor assembly which, like that of the FlexiBowl® 350, includes the entire kinematic assembly. In this case too, the spare part is the entire assembly.

### What you need

:::{list-table}
:widths: 40 40
:header-rows: 1

* - Item/equipment
  - Quantity

* - H2.5 hex key
  - 1

* - Replacement Backlight
  - 1

:::

### Standard motor assembly replacement

| Step | Operation |
|:----:|-----------|
| 1 | Remove the flip screen assembly |
| 2 | {ref}`Remove the surface or rigid disc <sost-disco>` |
| 3 | Remove the cover plate of the flip assembly (use two of the fixing screws for removal through the dedicated threaded holes) |
| 4 | Remove the pneumatic connections and remove the flip assembly |
| 5 | Replace the damaged flip assembly with the new one |
| 6 | Once the replacement is finished, refit all the components |
