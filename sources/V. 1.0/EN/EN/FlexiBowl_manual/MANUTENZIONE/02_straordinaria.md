# [MEC] **Extraordinary Maintenance**

:::{warning}
Extraordinary maintenance and repair of the machine are reserved for qualified, trained and authorized technicians, employed by the Manufacturer or by the authorized service center. These interventions require in-depth and specialist knowledge of the machines, of the necessary operations, of the associated risks and of the correct procedures for operating safely.
:::

If exceptional events occur that require extraordinary maintenance interventions, the user's routine maintenance technicians must follow these procedures: 
- check the condition of the damaged or misaligned assemblies; 
- carry out the operations described in this paragraph; 
- if the operations to be carried out are not covered in this manual, send the Manufacturer a report of the events that occurred, the result of the inspection and any observations. 

The Manufacturer or the authorized service center will assess the situation case by case. They will then agree with the routine maintenance technicians on the type of intervention to be carried out, choosing the most suitable solution among those listed below: 
- the Manufacturer sends an authorized, trained and qualified technician to carry out the necessary interventions; 
- or the Manufacturer authorizes the user's routine maintenance technicians to carry out the interventions, sending any additional instructions. 

:::{warning}
The spare parts to be replaced must be ordered from ARS S.r.l. 

If the customer does not use original spare parts or parts authorized in writing by the Manufacturer, the latter is released from any responsibility for the operation of the machine and for the safety of the operators. Authorization and/or instructions must always be communicated in writing. In the absence of written authorization, operating is prohibited and the Manufacturer declines all responsibility.
:::

:::{warning}
Maintenance operations must only be carried out by qualified and authorized personnel. 
:::
